# Code of conduct

This project is for musicians, teachers, students and developers of every
background and skill level. Everyone taking part (in issues, pull
requests, discussions and live sessions) is expected to:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming.
- Keep it safe for younger users: lessons may involve children, so no
  sexual content, no harassment and no sharing of anyone's personal
  details.

Not acceptable: harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats,
and sustained disruption.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the project owner via the contact details on
their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. The owner may
remove content, or block anyone who breaks these rules, from the
project's spaces.
