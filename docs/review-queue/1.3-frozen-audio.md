# 1.3 Frozen audio for missing plugins

Same `daw.exe`. Screenshots from the cloud, with the project's test plugin
removed after saving: `/mnt/project-files/screenshots/app-frozen-audio.png`
(the effects window on a computer without the plugin) and
`app-frozen-mixer.png` (the ❄ on the track's FX button). In the cloud the
track played from its frozen audio matched the track played through the
plugin to within one step of 24-bit.

- [ ] **1.3 Frozen audio is made.** In a saved project, put an amp sim
      on a guitar take, play with its settings, and save. The effects
      window says frozen audio is being made, then that it is made each
      time the project is saved. Pass: the project's `Audio` folder gains
      one new WAV per such track; it plays in another player and sounds
      like the track through the plugin (before its fader); saving again
      with nothing changed adds no file; changing a knob and saving
      replaces it (the old one is removed).
- [ ] **1.3 Frozen audio plays without the plugin.** Export a bundle of
      that project, then on a computer without the plugin (or after
      switching the plugin off in Settings → Plugins and moving its
      `.vst3` out of the folder), import it. Pass: the message says the
      plugin isn't installed and the track plays frozen audio, the FX
      button shows ❄, the track sounds as it did with the plugin, and its
      fader, pan, mute and solo still work.
- [ ] **1.3 The plugin survives the trip.** On that computer, change the
      track's volume, save, and bring the project back (bundle or shared
      folder) to the computer with the plugin. Pass: the plugin is there
      with the settings you left it with, in its place in the chain.
- [ ] **1.3 Out of date.** On the computer without the plugin, move the
      clip on that track. Pass: the effects window says the frozen audio
      no longer fits and the track plays without the plugin (dry), as
      it would before this feature.
- [ ] **1.3 Your plugins.** Note any plugin whose settings change each
      time they are read without being touched (it would make frozen
      audio again at every save, every 30 s while you work): the effects
      window keeps saying "Making frozen audio…" after saves where you
      changed nothing.
