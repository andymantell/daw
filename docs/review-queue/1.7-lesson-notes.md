# 1.7 Lesson notes

## Checks on the PC

- [ ] **1.7 Lesson notes and tab.** Screenshots:
      `app-lesson-notes-read.png` (notes shown formatted, with a tab
      block under Intro) and `app-lesson-notes-write.png` (the same notes
      being written, with the Heading, Bold and List buttons). They were
      drawn by the test harness, whose fonts lack a few icons, so the
      undo and redo buttons behind the window show as boxes. Add three
      markers, then Practice → Lesson notes…. Under Write, type a few
      lines; put the cursor on one and press Heading, select a word and
      press Bold, select two lines and press List. Press Read. Then
      press Add tab block, type a riff over the dashes and give it a
      title; add another and pick a different section for it. Also
      right-click a marker → Add tab for this section…. Press a block's
      Go to section and Loop section. Save, close and reopen. Pass: Read
      shows headings, bold words and lists, blocks are listed in song
      order under their section's name and bars, the buttons move the
      playhead or loop the section, and everything comes back after
      reopening. Notes written before this change still read as they
      were. Say whether typed marks (`#`, `**`, `-`) are fine for the
      teacher, or the notes should be edited already formatted.
- [ ] **1.7 Notes and tab between two computers.** With the teacher's
      copy in the shared folder, both of you add a tab block, and one of
      you moves a block to another section, then each opens the other's
      changes. Pass: both blocks are there on both computers and the move
      is kept. Known limit: if you both change the *same* block's text
      before syncing, the later change wins and the other is lost
      (notes themselves merge letter by letter). Say if that matters.
- [ ] **1.7 Commenters can read but not change notes.** Make the teacher
      a commenter (People…), and have them open the notes. Pass: they
      see Read only, with "Only editors can change lesson notes", and no
      Write, Add or Delete buttons.

## Decision

- [ ] **Deleted notes and tab out of the saved history?** Deleted
      comments are wiped from the saved file, its backup and bundles
      (see `1.6-comments.md`). Lesson notes and tab blocks are not yet:
      deleted or rewritten text stays readable in the file's history.
      Options: (a) do tab blocks the same way as comments (small: they
      are stored the same way); (b) also wipe deleted text from the
      notes themselves (more work, as the notes merge letter by letter;
      it means blanking each deleted letter in the history); (c) leave
      it. Recommendation: (a) and (b), since notes are where personal
      details are most likely to be typed. Until you say, nothing is
      wiped, and deleting a block can be undone.
