# 2.1 Fix a badly timed take (first cut)

Finds where each note starts in the selected clips, cuts the clips
there, and moves each note toward the nearest grid line. Grouped tracks
are cut and moved together, so drum mics stay in time with each other.

Screenshots: `screenshots/app-fix-timing.png` (the window before fixing,
with the notes found and where they'll go) and
`screenshots/app-fix-timing-done.png` (after: each take cut at its
notes on both grouped tracks).

## On the PC

- [ ] **2.1 Fixing a take.** Set the tempo, then record a few bars of
      eighth-note strumming or picking, a little loose. Select the take
      and choose **Edit → Fix timing…**. Set **Move notes to** to
      ½ beat. Pass: the window says how many notes it found, and the
      strip shows a tick at each note with a line to its grid line.
      Count the ticks against what you played.
- [ ] **2.1 Missed and extra notes.** If notes are missed, raise
      **Sensitivity**; if it finds notes that aren't there (such as a
      chord ringing), lower it. Pass: there's a setting where it finds
      your notes and nothing else. Note the setting that worked, and
      whether the default (60%) was close.
- [ ] **2.1 Moving the notes.** Press **Fix timing**. Pass: the take is
      cut at each note, the notes sit on the grid, and playing it back
      sounds tight, with no clicks, doubled attacks or gaps at the cuts.
      Edit → Undo puts the take back as it was, in one step.
- [ ] **2.1 Strength.** Undo, set **Strength** to 50% and fix again.
      Pass: notes move half way, so it still sounds played, not
      machine-like.
- [ ] **2.1 Grouped tracks.** Record (or import) the same part onto two
      tracks in one edit group (Edit → Edit groups…), say a close mic
      and a room mic. Select the close mic's take, choose that track
      in **Find notes on**, and fix. Pass: both takes are cut at the
      same places and moved by the same amounts, and they still sound
      in phase with each other.
- [ ] **2.1 Comps.** Try it on a take that's part of a comp. Pass: the
      window says comp pieces are left alone; after Flatten (in the
      Take lanes pane), fixing works.

## Decisions to accept

- [ ] **Filling the gaps.** Built: when a note moves later, the gap
      before it is filled by drawing that note's piece back over the
      end of the previous note's ring, with a 5 ms crossfade. The other
      way, stretching the earlier piece forward, would play the start
      of the moved note twice. When a note moves earlier, the earlier
      piece is shortened. No audio is stretched.
- [ ] **Defaults.** Built: ½ beat grid, 100% strength, 60%
      sensitivity. Two notes within 50 ms count as one, and two notes
      that would land on the same grid line stay together, uncut.
- [ ] **Its own grid.** Built: the window has its own grid setting
      rather than using the timeline's, since the timeline's is often
      "Auto". Say if you'd rather it followed the timeline.
- [ ] **Window, not timeline marks.** Built: the notes found are shown
      in the window's strip, not marked on the timeline. Marks on the
      timeline that you can drag or delete before fixing would be a
      later step.

## Not built yet

- Moving notes by stretching the audio instead of cutting it.
- Fixing comp pieces in place.
- Fixing toward a groove taken from another track, rather than the grid.
- Finding notes runs on the screen's thread; on a very long take there
  may be a short pause when the window opens or sensitivity changes.
