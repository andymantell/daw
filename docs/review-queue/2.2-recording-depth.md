# 2.2 Recording depth, part 1

Punch in and out, pre-roll and post-roll, recording many inputs at once,
and keeping what you played without Record on. Screenshots from the cloud
(test tones standing in for the interface), in
`/mnt/project-files/screenshots/`: `app-punch-setup.png`,
`app-punch-recording.png`, `app-punch-done.png`, `app-keep-last.png`,
`app-kept.png`.

- [ ] **2.2 Punch range with pre-roll and post-roll.** Load a backing
      track, arm a track on your guitar's input, and tick **Punch bars**
      on the fourth transport row (say bars 5 to 7, pre-roll 1 bar,
      post-roll 1 bar). Turn Record on and press Play from anywhere.
      Pass: playback starts at bar 4, the take covers exactly bars 5 to 7
      (it appears as soon as bar 7 is reached), and playback stops by
      itself at bar 8. With Count-in on as well, the count-in comes
      before bar 4. With Loop on (bars 4 to 8, say), each pass makes a
      new take of bars 5 to 7 and playback keeps going.
- [ ] **2.2 The punch lands in time.** With the recording offset measured
      (1.2), punch a click recorded through a loopback cable, bars 3 to
      4. Pass: the take starts and ends on the bar lines, within a
      millisecond, and there is no click missing at either end.
- [ ] **2.2 Punching in by hand.** Play with Record off, press R (or the
      Record button) part-way through, then again later. Pass: the take
      covers just the time Record was on, in time with the backing.
- [ ] **2.2 Many inputs at once, on the UMC1820.** Settings → Audio
      device, choose the UMC1820 with its inputs. In the track list press
      **+ A track for each input**: one armed track per input not
      already used appears, named "In 1", "In 2" and so on. Plug sources
      into several inputs (a mic, the guitar, a keyboard, or one source
      moved between them) and record a minute. Pass: each track has its
      own take with only its own input, all lined up, and the status bar
      never says "recording fell behind". Try at 64 and 128 samples too.
- [ ] **2.2 Keep what I just played.** With a track armed and Record
      **off**, play along for a while, then stop. Pass: the button next
      to **Always keep** counts up while playing ("Keep last 0:42"), and
      pressing it adds what you played as a new take where you played it,
      choosable in the track's Take list like any other. Looping, each
      pass is a take of its own. Pressing it twice doesn't make the same
      take again.

## Decisions to accept

- [ ] **How much "Always keep" holds, and when.** Built: on by default,
      keeping the last 2 minutes of each armed track's input while
      playing without recording (also the pre-roll and post-roll around a
      punch, so a good note just outside it isn't lost). The choices are
      Off, 30 seconds, 1, 2 and 5 minutes. It's kept in memory: about
      23 MB per mono track for 2 minutes, so 16 armed tracks for 5 minutes
      would be nearly 1 GB. Input while **stopped** isn't kept, because it
      has no place in the song; play over an empty part to keep an idea.
      What is kept is lost if the audio device is changed.
- [ ] **Punch settings aren't saved yet.** The punch bars, pre-roll and
      post-roll, and the Always keep choice last until the app closes.
      Say if they should be saved in the project (like the loop) or on
      this computer.
- [ ] **Recording buffer.** Raised so 16 mono tracks have about 10 seconds
      of slack if the disk stalls (85 seconds for one stereo track). It
      takes about 34 MB of memory whether recording or not.
