# 2.2 Take lanes and comping

Each pass of a loop recording is a take, shown one above another in the
**Take lanes** pane (View → Take lanes, or find "Take lanes" with
Ctrl+K). Drag across a take to use it there; the comp plays the chosen
parts with crossfades where they meet. Screenshots from the cloud (made-up
audio), in `/mnt/project-files/screenshots/`: `app-take-lanes.png`,
`app-take-lanes-comp.png`, `app-take-lanes-comps.png`.

- [ ] **2.2 Loop recording stacks takes.** Arm a track on your guitar,
      set the loop to 4 bars, turn on Loop and Record, and play the part
      four times through. Open View → Take lanes. Pass: four lanes,
      Take 4 on top, each lined up with the timeline above it (same zoom
      and scroll), and the newest take is what plays.
- [ ] **2.2 Swiping makes a comp.** Drag across bar 2 of Take 1, then
      bar 3 of Take 3. Pass: the comp row shows Take 4, Take 1, Take 3,
      Take 4 in turn; playback plays exactly those, with no click or gap
      at any join. Click (without dragging) on another take: that part
      of the comp switches to it. The take's button at the left uses it
      at the playhead.
- [ ] **2.2 Crossfades at the joins sound right.** Listen closely at a
      join in the middle of a held note and across a strummed chord.
      Built: each join is a 20 ms equal-power crossfade centred on it, and
      a cut end with nothing beside it fades over 5 ms. Pass: no clicks,
      no dip or bump in level.
- [ ] **2.2 Several comps.** With Comp 1 made, press **Duplicate**, change
      a part, then switch between Comp 1 and Comp 2 in the list (and back
      to "As recorded"). Pass: each plays as it was left; Rename and
      Delete work, and the takes always stay.
- [ ] **2.2 Comps are saved and undone.** Save, close and reopen the
      project, and press Ctrl+Z after a swipe. Pass: the comps come back
      as left, and undo takes back one swipe at a time.
- [ ] **2.2 Flatten.** Press **Flatten**. Pass: the track keeps just the
      comp's pieces as plain clips (named "Comp 1, take 3" and so on), the
      takes and comps go, and Ctrl+Z brings them back.
- [ ] **2.2 Recording over a comp.** With a comp playing, record another
      pass over part of it. Pass: the new take goes into the comp over
      the time it covers, and the rest of the comp is unchanged. Undo last
      take from the phone puts back the take that played there before.

## Decisions to accept

- [ ] **A pane under the timeline, not lanes inside it.** The takes show
      in their own pane, lined up with the timeline, rather than opening
      out under each track inside the timeline. That kept this work apart
      from the timeline's own code while other features change it. Say if
      lanes inside the timeline matter enough to build next.
- [ ] **Pieces are ordinary clips.** The comp plays as clips cut from the
      takes, so trimming, moving or deleting a piece on the timeline
      changes the comp, but a piece's own volume, fade or gain changes are
      lost the next time that comp is swiped or switched to.
- [ ] **Crossfade length is fixed.** 20 ms at each join, 5 ms at a cut
      end. Say if it should be a setting.
- [ ] **Not built yet: auditioning a take** (hearing one take on its own
      at a spot without changing the comp). Clicking a take changes the
      comp, and undo puts it back. Say if a separate listen button is
      wanted.
