# 2.3 Meters

Screenshot (a test signal standing in for a song):
`/mnt/project-files/screenshots/app-meters.png`.

The loudness, true peak and range readings are checked automatically
against the minimum-requirement test signals of the published loudness
meter specification that can be made from their descriptions (steady
tones, gating, the 400 ms and 3 s windows, highest readings in steps, the
true-peak sines, and the loudness range tones). Not covered: the 5-channel
test (the app is stereo), the tests that use the specification's own
programme recordings, and the true-peak tests built at four times the
sample rate. Those files are on the standard body's website if a check
against them is wanted later.

- [ ] **2.3 Strip meters.** Play a song. Each track's and the master's
      meter shows RMS as the solid bar and the peak as the paler bar
      beyond it. Pass: they move with the music, the RMS bar sits a few
      dB under the peak on most music, and they fall back smoothly when
      you stop.
- [ ] **2.3 Meters window.** Press "Meters…" in the master row (or click
      any meter). Play a song from the start. Pass: Momentary and
      Short-term move, Integrated settles after a few seconds, Range
      appears after a few seconds more, and the spectrum follows the
      music (bass on the left). Press Reset: the readings start again.
- [ ] **2.3 Compare with a meter you trust.** Export a finished mix (or
      take any commercial track as a WAV), and play it right through with
      the meters on Master. Compare Integrated, Range and True peak with
      the loudness meter in your other music software. Pass: Integrated within 0.1 LU, True peak within about
      0.2 dB, Range within 1 LU.
- [ ] **2.3 One track.** Choose a track under "Measure" (or click its
      meter). Pass: the readings are for that track alone, after its
      volume and pan; deleting that track switches back to Master.
- [ ] **2.3 Correlation.** A mono recording (a DI guitar) reads +1. A
      wide stereo mix sits somewhere between 0 and +1. Flip the phase of
      one side, if a plugin can, and it goes towards −1 (red). Pass: it
      matches what you hear when you switch your interface to mono.
- [ ] **2.3 Reset on play.** With "Reset on play" ticked, stop and play
      again: Integrated starts again. Untick it: it carries on across
      stops. Pass: that suits how you work; say if the other default
      would suit you better.
