# 2.3 The mixer as a mixing desk

Same `daw.exe`. The mixer pane is now a desk: one vertical strip per
track and bus in the timeline's order, the control groups and the master
pinned on the right. The old table is gone (Andy chose "desk only",
2026-11-01). Screenshots from the cloud, one per theme:
`/mnt/project-files/screenshots/mixer-desk/desk-valve-glow.png`,
`desk-workshop.png`, `desk-vellum.png`, `desk-tidal.png`,
`desk-valve-glow-light.png`, `desk-narrow.png` and
`desk-recording-layout.png` (the short mixer under the timeline).

Each strip, top to bottom: input (or a bus's name), arm R, monitor M and
practice mute, FX, Out, Sends, Group and Folder (when there are any),
the take; the pan pot; the fader with its dB scale and the meter beside
it on the same scale; the level as a number; Mute and Solo; the name tag
in the track's colour.

## On the PC

- [ ] **Look.** Open a band project in the Mixing layout. Pass: it reads
      as a desk, the strips are easy to tell apart, and the fader caps,
      scale and meters are clear in each of the four themes, light and
      dark. Say what to change per theme (the colours are `desk`,
      `fader_slot`, `fader_cap`, `fader_line`, `scribble` and
      `scribble_text` in the theme files).
- [ ] **Fader feel.** Drag a fader. Pass: it moves from where it is
      (no jump to the pointer), most of its throw is between -20 dB and
      +6 dB, it stops briefly at 0 dB, Shift-drag moves it finely, the
      wheel nudges it half a dB (Shift: a tenth), and a double-click puts
      it back to 0 dB. Is the throw long enough at your screen size?
- [ ] **Meters.** Play something loud. Pass: the meter beside each fader
      lines up with the scale (0 dB on the meter is level with the 0 dB
      mark), the peak line holds for a moment, and the clip light at the
      top stays lit after a clip until you click it. Clicking the meter
      still opens the meters window.
- [ ] **Pan.** Drag a pan pot up or right. Pass: it pans right, stops at
      the centre on the way through, and a double-click centres it.
- [ ] **Selected faders.** Click one name tag, Ctrl-click another, then
      drag either fader. Pass: both move by the same amount, keeping
      their levels apart. Click a tag again to clear the selection.
- [ ] **Narrow strips.** Press **Narrow strips** (or search for the
      command). Pass: only pan, fader, meter, mute, solo and name show,
      so more strips fit.
- [ ] **Short pane.** In the Recording layout, where the mixer is short,
      each strip keeps FX, R, M and practice mute and folds the rest of
      its routing (input, out, sends, group, folder, take) into a **▾**
      menu beside FX. Shorter still, the desk starts scrolled to its foot
      so the faders show; scroll up for the rest. Does that work for you,
      or would you rather the Recording layout gave the mixer more room?
- [ ] **Keyboard and screen reader.** Tab to a fader. Pass: it is read as
      "<track> volume, -6.0 dB" as before; Up and Down step it half a dB
      (Shift: a tenth); the number box under it ("<track> level in dB")
      takes a typed level.

## For assistants

Nothing changes in the tools: volume, pan, mute and solo are still
`set_parameter`, routing `edit_track` and `set_send`. `get_selection`
now also says which strips are selected (`mixer_strips`) and whether the
strips are narrow (`mixer_narrow`); the `mixer-narrow` command switches
it.
