# 2.3 Mixer routing and delay compensation

Same `daw.exe`. Screenshots from the cloud:
`/mnt/project-files/screenshots/app-mixer-routing.png` (a folder, a bus,
a control group and the Out, Sends and Group columns),
`app-mixer-sends.png` (a track's Sends menu) and
`app-mixer-sidechain.png` (the compressor listening to another track).

The engine proves the routing by null tests (two ways of routing the
same sound must come out the same, sample for sample), so these checks
are about how it feels and sounds on real material.

## On the PC

- [ ] **2.3 A reverb return.** In the mixer press **+ Add bus**, name it
      "Reverb", open its **FX** and add a Reverb or Room reverb. On a
      guitar track open **Sends → Add send to… → Reverb** and set its
      level. Pass: the guitar plays dry with the reverb on top; turning
      the send down takes the reverb away; **Mute** on the send does the
      same; **Pre** keeps the reverb at the same level while the guitar's
      own volume fader moves.
- [ ] **2.3 A folder.** Press **+ Add folder**, name it "Band", and put
      two tracks in it with the **No folder** picker on each. Pass: the
      tracks move under the folder in the mixer and the timeline (with
      the folder's name tagged "folder"), and the folder's volume and
      mute act on both. Put the Room reverb on the folder: both tracks go
      through it.
- [ ] **2.3 Out.** Set a track's **Out** to a bus, then back to
      **Master** (or **Folder**). Pass: no click or dropout at the
      change, and a bus can't be picked for a track whose audio already
      reaches it (the pick is missing from the list).
- [ ] **2.3 Control group.** Press **+ Add control group**, put two
      tracks in it with the **Group** picker, and move the group's fader
      under **Control groups**. Pass: both tracks follow it while playing,
      keeping their own levels apart; **Mute** silences both; a track's
      own fader still works on its own.
- [ ] **2.3 Sidechain.** On a backing track add a **Compressor**, set
      **Listen** to the vocal or lead track, and lower **Threshold**.
      Pass: the backing ducks when the lead plays, in time with it, and
      "Turning down" moves with the lead, not the backing.
- [ ] **2.3 Delay compensation.** Put the **Limiter** (72 smp) or a
      plugin with latency on a bus that a recorded take goes to, and
      send the same track to a return with a slow plugin. Pass: the take
      stays in time with the metronome and the other tracks, and
      Settings → Performance shows no overloads.
- [ ] **2.3 Saved and reopened.** Save, close and reopen. Pass: the
      buses, folders, sends, groups and sidechains are as they were, and
      Undo takes each routing change back one step.
- [ ] **2.3 Stems.** Export with stems. Pass: each track's stem is that
      track alone, through its own effects and fader, without its bus or
      sends (buses get a stem of their own).

## Decisions

- [ ] **2.3 What a muted track's pre-fader send does.** Now a track's
      Mute (and solo on another track) silences its pre-fader sends too,
      so muting really silences it. Some mixers let pre-fader sends go
      on; say if you want that.
- [ ] **2.3 Where sidechains come from.** The compressor listens to the
      other track after its effects and fader. Say if you would rather
      it listened before the fader, so turning the lead down doesn't
      change the ducking.
