# 2.3 Monitor section

Same `daw.exe`. **View → Monitor section** opens the pane. Screenshots
from the cloud: `/mnt/project-files/screenshots/app-monitor-section.png`
(two headphone mixes and the speakers' controls) and
`app-monitor-setup.png` (where the speakers, headphones and talkback
microphone are plugged in).

The engine proves the headphone mixes by null tests
(`crates/engine/tests/monitor.rs`): a mix with its own levels matches the
main mix made with those levels, changes to the main mix leave it alone,
and it stays in time with the speakers through buses with slow effects.
These checks are about the interface's real outputs, and how it feels.

## On the PC

- [ ] **2.3 Plug in.** Open **Outputs and talkback microphone (this
      computer)** at the bottom of the pane. Add a headphone mix with
      **+ Add headphone mix** and set **Plays on** to the outputs your
      headphone amp (or a pair of line outputs you can hear) is plugged
      into. Pass: the mix plays there and nowhere else; the speakers keep
      the main mix; the choice is still there after restarting the app.
- [ ] **2.3 A mix of their own.** Play a song with two or three tracks.
      In the headphone mix, turn one track down, pan one, and mute one
      (**M**). Pass: the headphones change, the speakers don't. Now move a
      track's volume fader, mute it or solo another in the mixer. Pass:
      the headphones don't change.
- [ ] **2.3 Two performers.** Add a second mix on other outputs, and give
      it different levels. Pass: each pair of headphones has its own mix,
      and each mix's meter moves with it.
- [ ] **2.3 In time.** Put a slow effect (the Limiter, or a plugin with
      latency) on a bus a track goes to, and have the bus in the
      headphone mix. Record along with the headphones on. Pass: the take
      lands in time, as it does when listening to the speakers.
- [ ] **2.3 Click.** Turn the metronome on. Set **Click** in one mix to
      −12 dB and untick it in the other. Pass: louder, quieter and no
      click, in the right headphones; the speakers' click is unchanged.
- [ ] **2.3 Same as main.** Tick **Same as main** on a mix. Pass: those
      headphones play what the speakers play (before **Dim**, **Mono** and
      the speaker level), and the mix's own level still works.
- [ ] **2.3 Talkback.** Choose the **Talkback microphone** input, then
      hold **🎙 Talk** and speak. Pass: you are heard in the headphones of
      mixes with **Talkback** ticked, not in the others, never on the
      speakers, and the speakers drop by the talkback dim (20 dB) while
      you hold it. Tick **Latch**: one click turns it on, another off. No
      click or thump as it comes on or off.
- [ ] **2.3 Dim, Mono, Cut.** Press each while playing. Pass: **Dim**
      drops the speakers by 20 dB (the amount is under the setup),
      **Mono** puts left and right together, **Cut** silences the
      speakers, and the headphones are unaffected by all three. No
      clicks.
- [ ] **2.3 Listen to a mix.** Set **Listen to** to a headphone mix.
      Pass: the speakers play that performer's mix; back to **Main mix**
      plays the main mix again.
- [ ] **2.3 Switching speakers.** If you have a second pair of speakers
      (or headphones on line outputs to stand in), add them with **+ Add
      speakers**, set their outputs and a trim. Pass: the buttons beside
      **Cut** switch between the pairs with a short fade, only one pair
      plays at a time, and the trim evens out their loudness.
- [ ] **2.3 Keys.** In Settings → Key commands, give keys to **Talkback
      on or off**, **Dim the speakers** and **Mono on or off** (they are
      also in the Transport menu). Pass: each works while playing.
- [ ] **2.3 Saved.** Save, close and reopen the project. Pass: the
      headphone mixes, their levels and settings are back; Undo takes a
      level change back one step. The outputs and speakers belong to the
      computer, so they come back on this PC with any project.

## Decisions

- [ ] **2.3 Where the headphone mixes take each track.** Now after the
      track's effects and pan, before its volume fader, mute and solo, so
      the main mix never changes what a performer hears. Say if you would
      rather mixes followed the track's mute, or its fader.
- [ ] **2.3 Buses in a headphone mix.** A new mix has every audio track
      at 0 dB and every bus and folder off, so nothing is heard twice
      (a folder's tracks are already in). **Copy main mix** does the same
      for buses that tracks go through, and keeps effect returns (a
      reverb) at their fader. Say if you would like it otherwise.
- [ ] **2.3 What restarts.** The speaker level, Dim, Mono, Cut, which
      speakers play and talkback all start fresh each time the app opens
      (full level, stereo, talkback off), so nothing is left dimmed or
      talking by surprise. Say if you would rather the level and the
      speakers chosen were kept.
