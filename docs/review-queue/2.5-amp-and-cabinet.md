# 2.5 Guitar amp, speaker cabinets and amp captures

Same `daw.exe`. Screenshots from the cloud:
`/mnt/project-files/screenshots/app-guitar-amp.png` and
`app-amp-capture.png`. Built at risk; nothing here has been heard on a
real guitar yet.

## On the PC

- [ ] **2.5 Picking them.** Click **FX** on a track and open **Add
      effect…**. Pass: **Guitar amp**, **Speaker cabinet** and **Amp
      capture** are in the "Built in" group.
- [ ] **2.5 Playing through the amp live.** Arm a track with the guitar
      plugged into the interface, put **Guitar amp** on it and monitor.
      Pass: no crackles, and the delay feels the same as without it (the
      amp adds none). Settings → Performance shouldn't show overloads
      (it takes about 7% of one core in the cloud's measurement).
- [ ] **2.5 Your own cabinet file.** In **Speaker cabinet** choose
      **Your own file…** and pick a cabinet impulse response WAV. Pass:
      you hear it, at about the level of the cabinets made here, and it
      is kept with the project after saving.
- [ ] **2.5 A capture file.** Download a free capture (`.nam`) of an amp
      you like, add **Amp capture**, press **Choose…** and pick it. Pass:
      the name and who made it show, it sounds like the amp, and after
      saving and reopening (or moving the original file) it still plays.
      If the line says it has no speaker cabinet, add **Speaker cabinet**
      after it.
- [ ] **2.5 A capture at another sample rate.** Run the project at
      44.1 kHz with a capture made at 48 kHz. Pass: it sounds the same as
      at 48 kHz, and a recorded take through it stays in time (it reports
      a delay of about 65 samples, made up on the track).
- [ ] **2.5 Capture levels.** With **Even out loudness** on, two
      different captures play at about the same loudness; with it off,
      at the level the file makes.

## Listening

- [ ] **2.5 The amp's voices.** Clean, Warm, Crunch and High gain, with
      Gain from 0 to 10. Pass: each sounds like a usable guitar amp (not
      fizzy, harsh or thin), turning Gain up adds grit more than
      loudness, and switching voice doesn't jump much in level.
- [ ] **2.5 The amp's tone controls.** Bass, Middle, Treble and Presence
      each do what they say across their range.
- [ ] **2.5 Cabinets.** The five cabinets made here, in the amp and in
      **Speaker cabinet**: each sounds like a speaker in a box, and they
      differ in a useful way. If none sounds right, say which way it is
      off (boxy, dull, fizzy, thin); the shapes are easy to change.
