# 2.5 Built-in effects

Same `daw.exe`. Screenshots from the cloud:
`/mnt/project-files/screenshots/app-builtin-dynamics.png` and
`app-builtin-reverb.png`.

## On the PC

- [ ] **2.5 Picking one.** Click **FX** on a track and open **Add
      effect…**. Pass: a "Built in" group lists EQ, Compressor, Noise
      gate, Limiter, Delay, Reverb and Room reverb above your plugins, and
      typing in Search narrows both.
- [ ] **2.5 Saved and reopened.** Add a few built-in effects, change some
      settings, save, close and reopen the project. Pass: the same
      effects in the same order with the same settings, and it sounds the
      same. Change a setting and press Undo: it goes back.
- [ ] **2.5 No glitches.** While a backing track plays, drag each
      effect's sliders, switch the Room reverb's **Room**, and toggle
      **On**. Pass: no crackles or dropouts beyond the change itself
      (switching room fades over about 5 ms). Settings → Performance
      shouldn't show overloads with a few on each track.
- [ ] **2.5 Limiter latency.** Put the Limiter on a recorded take's track
      (it shows "72 smp" at 48 kHz). Pass: the take still plays in time
      with the backing.

## Listening

- [ ] **2.5 EQ.** On a guitar or full mix: Bass, the two middle bands and
      Treble each do what they say, Low cut takes out rumble, High cut
      takes out hiss. Pass: no harshness or odd peaks at extreme settings.
- [ ] **2.5 Compressor.** On a clean guitar or vocal with uneven playing:
      lower **Threshold** until "Turning down" reads 3 to 6 dB. Pass: it
      evens out without pumping at the default attack and release.
- [ ] **2.5 Noise gate.** On a guitar with amp hiss: set **Threshold**
      just above the hiss. Pass: silence between notes, notes not cut off
      or chattering as they fade (raise **Hold** or **Release** if they
      are, and say what worked).
- [ ] **2.5 Limiter.** On the loudest part of a mix with **Drive** at 6
      to 10 dB. Pass: louder with no clipping or obvious distortion.
- [ ] **2.5 Delay.** Try **Bounce left and right** and **Tone**. Pass:
      clean echoes; changing **Time** while playing pitches the echoes
      rather than clicking.
- [ ] **2.5 Reverb.** Try short and long **Decay** and low **Tone**. Pass:
      smooth, without metallic ringing or flutter on drums or plucked
      notes. Say if it sounds too thin or too bright.
- [ ] **2.5 Room reverb.** Listen to each **Room** (Small room, Studio,
      Hall, Plate). Pass: each sounds like a believable space. These
      responses are made up from decaying noise rather than recorded, so
      say which sound fake and whether loading your own response files
      should come sooner.
