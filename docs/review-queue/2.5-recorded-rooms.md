# 2.5 Recorded rooms for the Room reverb

Same `daw.exe`. Screenshots from the cloud:
`/mnt/project-files/screenshots/app-recorded-room.png`,
`app-recorded-room-list.png` and `app-sound-credits.png`.

Fifteen rooms come with the app: twelve real spaces from the OpenAIR
library (University of York) and three spring reverbs from Adventure Kid,
all CC BY 4.0. Licences and what was left out are in
[decision 0011](../decisions/0011-bundled-reverb-rooms.md).

## On the PC

- [ ] **2.5 Recorded rooms.** Put a Room reverb on a guitar track and open
      its **Room** list. Under "Recorded spaces" try a few: Concert hall,
      Cathedral, Stairwell, Railway tunnel, and the three Springs. Each
      should sound like its name, with no click at the start and no
      sudden cut at the end. The line under the list credits the room.
- [ ] **2.5 Rooms in a shared project.** Save a project using a recorded
      room and open it on another computer (or send it as a bundle): the
      same room plays, and the project folder holds no copy of it.
- [ ] **2.5 Sound credits.** Help → Sound credits lists all fifteen, each
      with who recorded it, the licence and the source page.

## Your call

- [ ] Are these the right fifteen? More can be added from the same
      places (OpenAIR has around 40 spaces), each costing about 0.2–2 MB.
