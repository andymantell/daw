# 2.6 Automation lanes

Same `daw.exe`. Screenshot from the cloud:
`/mnt/project-files/screenshots/app-automation.png` (the Automation
pane under the timeline, with a volume lane on a track in Touch and
the fader showing the lane's value).

Any setting of a track (volume, pan, a send, a built-in effect's
setting or a plugin parameter) can have a lane: a line of points the
setting follows as the song plays. The pane is **View → Automation**.
Each track has a mode: **Off** (the lanes are ignored), **Read** (the
controls follow their lanes), **Touch** (a control you hold while
playing is written, and goes back to its line when you let go),
**Latch** (written from the first touch until you stop) and **Write**
(every lane on the track is written from play to stop, with the
controls as they are). The engine proves by test that volume, pan, send
and effect lanes are followed, so these checks are about how it feels.

## On the PC

- [ ] **2.6 Drawing a lane.** Open **View → Automation**, pick a track
      with a recorded take, press **+ Lane → Volume**. Pass: a flat
      line at the fader's level appears. Click on the lane to add
      points (they snap to the grid unless Alt is held), drag them,
      right-click one to delete it. Play: the fader in the mixer moves
      along the line and the sound follows, with no clicks or steps.
- [ ] **2.6 Touch.** Set the mode to **Touch**, play, and hold the
      track's fader while moving it for a few bars, then let go. Pass:
      while held the lane's readout says "writing" and the sound follows
      your hand; on letting go the line shows what you did and the fader
      jumps back to the line. Points before and after the pass are as
      they were. Undo takes the pass back in one step.
- [ ] **2.6 Latch and Write.** In **Latch**, touch the fader once and
      let go: it keeps writing the value you left it at until Stop. In
      **Write**, press play without touching anything: every lane on the
      track is overwritten with the controls' values until Stop. Pass:
      each behaves as said, and the lane shows a level line over the
      stretch played.
- [ ] **2.6 A control without a lane.** In Touch, with no pan lane,
      drag the pan while playing. Pass: a Pan lane appears in the pane
      with the move in it.
- [ ] **2.6 An effect's setting.** Put the built-in **Compressor** on a
      track, open its **Controls**, and in Touch move **Threshold**
      while playing. Pass: a "Compressor: Threshold" lane is written,
      and in Read the Threshold slider follows it. The same with a send
      level in the **Sends** menu.
- [ ] **2.6 A plugin's parameter.** On a track with a plugin, **+ Lane**
      → one of the plugin's parameters, draw a few points, play. Pass:
      the plugin's own window shows the parameter moving. In Touch,
      move that parameter in the plugin's window while playing: it is
      written, and the pass ends about half a second after you stop
      moving it (the plugin's window gives no other sign of letting go).
- [ ] **2.6 Export.** Export a mix with a volume lane and an effect
      lane. Pass: the exported file follows both. A plugin lane is not
      followed by export yet (see below): say if that matters before
      the rest of plugin depth (2.4) is built.
- [ ] **2.6 Saved and shared.** Save, close and reopen; open on the
      second computer. Pass: the lanes are there, and **Snapshots**
      lists lane changes as "Automation changed".
- [ ] **2.6 Cost.** With lanes on several tracks, Settings →
      Performance shows no overloads while playing at a 128-sample
      buffer.

## Decisions

- [ ] **2.6 Where the mode lives.** The mode is per track and is
      remembered only while the app is open: a reopened project starts
      every track in Read, so nothing is written over by accident. Say
      if you want it saved with the project.
- [ ] **2.6 Plugin parameters.** Plugin lanes are driven from the
      window thread (about sixty times a second), not sample by sample
      as the others are, and an export doesn't follow them. Doing it in
      the engine needs the plugins' process-side parameter path (the
      rest of 2.4). Say whether to do that next, or leave it.
- [ ] **2.6 Lanes in the timeline.** The lanes live in their own pane
      (like take lanes), lined up with the timeline, rather than
      unfolding under each track in the timeline itself. Say if you want
      them under the tracks too.
