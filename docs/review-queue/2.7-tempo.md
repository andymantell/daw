# 2.7 Tempo and time signature changes

## On the PC

- [ ] **2.7 Tempo changes you can hear.** Screenshots: `app-tempo-ruler.png`
      (the ruler with changes) and `app-tempo-window.png` (the window).
      Open a project, press **Changes…** next to Tempo in the transport
      bar, and add a tempo change at bar 5 set to 140 BPM. Turn the
      metronome on and play from bar 3. Pass: the clicks speed up exactly
      on bar 5, bar 5's first click is the accented one, and the ruler
      shows a "140 BPM" label on bar 5 with the bar lines after it closer
      together.
- [ ] **2.7 Glides.** Tick **Glide** on that change. Pass: from bar 1 the
      clicks speed up smoothly, reaching 140 on bar 5, with no jump or
      stutter, and a green slope on the ruler shows the glide. Untick it:
      back to a jump.
- [ ] **2.7 Time signature changes.** Add a time signature change at bar
      9, set to 6/8 (or 7/8). Pass: from bar 9 the metronome accents every
      sixth (or seventh) eighth note, the bar numbers on the ruler and the
      position readout ("9.1.000") follow it, and Snap to Bar lands on
      the new bar lines.
- [ ] **2.7 Count-in at the new tempo.** With count-in on, put the
      playhead on bar 9 and press Play. Pass: the count-in bar is at the
      tempo and time signature of bar 9, not the bars before it, and the
      song comes in on the beat.
- [ ] **2.7 The loop and markers stay on their bars.** Add markers at
      bars 5 and 9, loop bars 5 to 9, then change the tempo change's BPM
      while looping. Pass: the loop and markers stay on the same bars
      (they move in seconds, not in bars), and the loop wraps cleanly.
      Recorded or imported audio stays where it is in seconds, so it
      drifts from the bars after a tempo change: that is expected for now
      (audio that follows the tempo is later work).
- [ ] **2.7 Right-click on the ruler.** Right-click the ruler between the
      bar numbers. Pass: the menu offers "Add tempo change at bar N, beat
      B…", "Add time signature change at bar N…" and "Tempo and time
      signature…", and each opens the window with the change added.
- [ ] **2.7 Saved, shared and undone.** Save, close and reopen; then open
      the project on the second computer (bundle or shared folder).
      Pass: the changes are all there. Edit → Undo takes back each edit
      to a change, one at a time.

## Decisions to accept

- [ ] **How a glide moves.** Built: the tempo moves evenly per beat, so
      halfway through a glide's beats it is halfway between the two
      tempos. The other common choice moves evenly per second (it spends
      longer at the slow end). Say if you want that, or both as a choice.
- [ ] **Changes go by bar and beat.** Like markers, tempo changes sit on
      a bar and beat, and time signature changes on a bar line, so a
      change earlier in the song never knocks a later one off its beat.
      Changes between beats aren't offered.
- [ ] **The start stays in the transport bar.** The tempo and time
      signature at bar 1 are still the transport bar's Tempo and Time
      signature boxes; the window only holds changes after that. A change
      put on bar 1 beat 1, or two on the same beat, is shown as "Not used"
      with the reason.
- [ ] **The start's beat unit.** The transport bar now has a beat unit
      (the "/ 4") next to beats per bar, so a song can start in 6/8.

Known limits: audio clips are placed in seconds, so they don't follow a
tempo change (the timing model allows clips that follow the tempo; that
comes with editing work in 2.1). Plugins aren't told the tempo yet.
There's no tempo lane to draw on; changes are edited in the window.
