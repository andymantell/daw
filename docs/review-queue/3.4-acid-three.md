# 3.4 Acid Three (classic synths, S2)

The acid bass line synth (`crates/instruments/src/acid.rs`, plan
`docs/plans/classic-synths.md`): one oscillator, saw or square, into
the vintage toolkit's four-pole diode ladder, a filter envelope with
attack and decay only, an amplifier envelope with a fixed decay, and
the two behaviours that make its lines move:

- **Accent** comes from velocity: a note played at or above the
  "Accent from velocity" setting (100 by default) is louder, its filter
  envelope decays quickly however Decay is set, and an extra sweep is
  added to the cutoff that piles up over consecutive accents and grows
  with the resonance, as the circuit's accent capacitor does.
- **Slide** comes from overlap: a note that starts while the one before
  is still held glides into it over the Slide time with no envelope
  restart. So patterns and note clips drive it with nothing but notes.

In the app: **Edit → Add Acid Three track** (`add-acid-three-track` in
Ctrl+K), "Acid Three" in the instrument list, `acid-three` to assistants
(`add_track`, and every setting under `instrument.` in
`list_parameters`, `set_parameters` and automation).

## Listening

The renders are in the project files under `synths/listening/`
(`cargo run -p daw-instruments --release --example acid_listen -- <dir>`
makes them again). Each is normalised to the same peak. All play the
same sixteen-step line at 128 BPM with accents and slides, driven by
note events as a clip would send them.

- [ ] **The squelch.** `acid-square-sweep.wav`: the square wave, four
      passes with the cutoff opening and the resonance rising. Pass: it
      squelches, the accents jump out and get brighter when they come
      in a row (steps 1, 5, 9, 12 and 15 are accented), the slides
      (into steps 4, 7, 12 and 16) bend rather than step.
- [ ] **The saw with drive.** `acid-saw-drive.wav`: the saw, a longer
      decay and Drive at 35%. Pass: hollower than the square at the
      bottom, growling on the accents, no fizz.
- [ ] **Accents alone.** `acid-accents.wav`: one note sixteen times:
      eight plain, then every other one accented, then four accented in
      a row. Pass: the accented notes are plainly louder (about 4 dB)
      and brighter, and the last four climb as the sweep piles up.
- [ ] **Slides alone.** `acid-slides.wav`: octave slides up and down
      at the default 60 ms, then the same at 250 ms. Pass: the pitch
      glides and the note does not restart; the slow one sounds like a
      portamento, the fast one like the machine.
- [ ] **Resonance steps.** `acid-resonance-steps.wav`: a held low note
      with the envelope sweeping, at resonance 0, 30, 60, 80, 95 and
      100%. Pass: the bass thins as the resonance rises and the ladder
      sings at the top. Say if the level change across the steps (held
      about even to 80%, louder when it sings) is right.
- [ ] **In the app.** Add an Acid Three track, draw a line of
      sixteenths with a few at velocity 110 and a couple overlapping
      the next, play it. Pass: accents and slides as in the renders;
      the controls are in the instrument window, in Ctrl+K and reach an
      assistant.

## What is built in, and why

- The diode ladder is the toolkit's (`vintage::diode`), driven gently
  (the oscillator at 0.3 of full scale), with the feedback high-pass at
  60 Hz and 30% of the bass the feedback takes put back at its input.
  The ladder loses level as resonance rises; some is given back after
  it (0.1 per unit of feedback), so a line stays about as loud from
  resonance 0 to 80% and gets louder only when it sings.
- Envelopes: filter attack 3 ms, decay from the knob (50 ms to 3 s,
  stretched threefold inside so the knob reads as where the sweep is
  mostly over); accented notes decay in 200 ms whatever the knob says.
  Amplifier: 3 ms attack, 2.5 s decay, 12 ms release at the gate's end.
- Accent: adds `accent × (0.5 + 0.5 × resonance)` to a pile capped at
  two accents' worth; the pile drains with a 250 ms time constant
  (slower than the accented note's own decay, which is the "wow"), a
  plain note halves it, and the sweep that follows the pile opens the
  cutoff up to 1.5 octaves per unit and raises the level by up to 5 dB.
- Slide: the toolkit's constant-time glide; legato through the shared
  voice allocator, last-note priority.
- Vintage drift (30% by default) wanders the pitch a few cents.

## Measured

Golden spectrum (test `a_fixed_patch_renders_to_its_known_spectrum`):
the square at A1 through cutoff 400 Hz, resonance 70%, envelope 60%,
decay 300 ms, accented; harmonics 1, 3, 5 and 7 over the second quarter
second are pinned to within 1.5 dB. CPU: one voice is 1.1% of one core
at 48 kHz in a release build on the cloud test machine (the test
`a_voice_costs_little` bounds it at 5%).

## Decisions to accept

- [x] **Decay convention.** The Decay knob (50 ms to 3 s) is where the
      sweep is about 90% done, so a setting of 300 ms sounds like a
      300 ms sweep. The other synths use the toolkit's convention (the
      time to 99.9%). Keep the stretch for this one?
- [x] **Accent velocity.** Accent is at or above velocity 100 by default
      (a setting from 1 to 127). The note editor's velocity tools and
      the assistant's `velocity` field set it. Is 100 the right default?
