# 3.4 Built-in instruments

Same `daw.exe`. Built at risk while the owner was away. Nothing here has
been played from a real keyboard or heard on the PC yet. The sound code
is covered by tests in `crates/instruments`, extra outputs by
`crates/engine/tests/instrument_outputs.rs`, and the windows by kittest
tests in `crates/app/src/instrument.rs`. Screenshots from the cloud
build are in the project's `screenshots/` folder (`app-synth.png`,
`app-sampler.png`, `app-drum-machine*.png`, `app-loop-slicer.png`).

## What was built

A new `instruments` crate with five instruments. The command search
(Ctrl+K) adds a track for each: *Synth*, *Sampler*, *Drum
sampler*, *Drum machine* and *Loop slicer*. The instrument button on the
track opens its window.

- **Synth** (our own): two oscillators (sine, triangle, saw, square,
  pulse), a sub oscillator, noise, up to 4 unison voices with
  spread, a low/band/high-pass filter with its own envelope, key
  tracking and an LFO, an amp envelope, and mono mode with glide. 16
  voices.
- **Sampler** (multi-zone): add many sound files at once; each becomes
  a zone at the root note its name gives (`C3`, `F#2`…), or on the next
  keys. **Spread** shares the keyboard between the zones. Each zone has
  key and velocity ranges, tuning, level, pan and a loop.
- **Drum sampler**: one sound on a key, with tuning, start, decay,
  reverse, a filter, *cut itself off*, and one-shot or held playing.
- **Drum machine**: 16 pads from C2 (note 36). Each pad layers up to 4
  sounds (a file, or one of 9 drums made in code), each with its own
  velocity range, level, tuning, pan, decay and tone. Pads have choke
  groups (the hats choke each other by default), their own effects
  (any built-in effect, on the **Pad effects** tab), two sends, and an
  output: main, or one of 16 extra outputs. **Put each pad on a bus**
  makes a bus for each pad in use and routes it there.
- **Loop slicer**: load a loop, cut it at the hits (with a sensitivity)
  or into equal parts. Each slice plays on its own key from C2, with
  its own tuning, pan, low-pass filter and *backwards* switch (click a
  slice on the waveform, or pick it from **Slice**). **Make notes that
  play the loop** writes a note clip that plays the slices on their
  beats, so the loop follows the song's tempo.

Instruments, their sound files (copied into the project's assets on
save), pad effects and extra output routes are saved in the project.
Extra outputs reach their bus lined up with the track, through delay
compensation.

Every action is a command, so it works from key commands, the command
search and scripts: `add-synth-track`, `add-sampler-track`,
`add-drum-sampler-track`, `add-drum-machine-track`, `add-slicer-track`,
`instrument-window`, `slice-notes`, `pad-buses`.

## Decisions for you

- [ ] **Which sounds ship.** Nothing third-party ships. The drum
      machine's default kit is drums made in code, and **Made here** in
      every sound menu has a few made sounds (keys, pluck, kick, snare,
      hat, a one-bar loop) for trying things. Say what sound content
      you want, if any.
- [ ] **Pad effects with latency aren't lined up.** Built-in effects
      with look-ahead on a pad make that pad a little late. Pass: fine
      for now, or say if it matters.
- [ ] **No round-robin yet.** Layers pick by velocity only; repeated
      hits of the same sample sound identical.
- [ ] **No clicking pads to hear them yet.** Play them from a keyboard
      or notes.
- [ ] **Pad effects are built-in effects only** (no containers, plugins
      or effects that need a file, such as recorded rooms).

## On the PC

- [ ] **Synth.** Add a synth track, play chords and a bass line with
      mono and glide on. Pass: no clicks or zipper noise when moving
      the filter, no stuck notes.
- [ ] **Sampler.** Drop several sound files named by note into the
      sampler and **Spread** them. Pass: each key plays the nearest
      sample at the right pitch, and loops don't click.
- [ ] **Drum machine.** Play the default kit from a pad controller or
      keyboard. Pass: the closed hat cuts the open hat, layered snare
      sounds like one hit, levels are roughly even. Then **Put each pad
      on a bus** and check each pad shows on its own bus, in time.
- [ ] **Loop slicer.** Load a drum loop, **Cut at the hits**, then
      **Make notes that play the loop** and change the song tempo.
      Pass: the loop follows the tempo without glitches, and the hits
      were found where you'd expect.
- [ ] **Saving.** Save, close and reopen a project with all five
      instruments. Pass: everything sounds the same.
