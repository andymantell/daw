# 3.4 Model Mono (classic synths, S1)

The three-oscillator ladder monosynth from the classic synths plan
(`docs/plans/classic-synths.md`), for bass and leads: three free-running
oscillators with six footages and six shapes, the third doubling as a
modulation source at a fixed rate; white or pink noise; a mixer that
overdrives as its levels and the overdrive control rise; the 24 dB
transistor ladder with emphasis, keyboard tracking off, ⅓, ⅔ or full,
and its own contour; a loudness contour; a switch that makes the
release take the decay's time; glide at a constant rate; low, high or
last note priority; single or multiple triggering; and the mod wheel
to pitch and filter. It is "Model Mono" in the instrument list,
`model-mono` to assistants, `add-model-mono-track` in Ctrl+K.

Every setting is in the shared command table (`instrument.<key>` to
`set_parameter`, `set_parameters` and the automation lanes), the
sounds made in code are listed by `get_instrument` with the settings
that make them (an assistant gives them to `set_instrument`), and the
end-to-end test `the_model_mono_is_played_through_the_assistant` adds
the track, voices the fat bass, plays a line and renders it.

## Listening

The renders are in the project files under `synths/listening/`
(`cargo run -p daw-instruments --release --example model_mono_listen -- <dir>`
makes them again). Each is normalised to the same peak. They are the
eight sounds made in code, each playing a short line, and three
demonstrations.

- [ ] **The basses.** `model-mono-fat-bass.wav` (two saws at 16' with a
      square sub at 32'), `model-mono-round-bass.wav` (triangle and a
      low square, little emphasis) and `model-mono-growl-bass.wav` (saw,
      shark tooth and a narrow pulse, the mixer hot, emphasis at 55%).
      Pass: weight and a filter sweep that sings rather than the old
      "farty" wah; say which of the three is nearest what the deep house
      bass needed, and whether the default **Mixer overdrive** (25%) is
      too clean or too hot.
- [ ] **The leads and keys.** `model-mono-screaming-lead.wav` (three saws
      spread, glide, highest-note priority), `model-mono-vibrato-lead.wav`
      (oscillator 3 as a 6 Hz triangle into the pitch), `model-mono-brass.wav`
      (slow filter attack) and `model-mono-soft-keys.wav` (triangle, every
      note retriggers). Pass: the leads cut and the glide sounds like a
      constant-rate glide (a wide jump takes longer); the vibrato is a
      vibrato and not a trill.
- [ ] **Overdrive rising.** `model-mono-overdrive-rising.wav`: the fat
      bass with the mixer overdrive going from clean to full over the
      line. Pass: it thickens and growls as it goes without fizz; say
      where on the way it sounds best, to set the default.
- [ ] **Emphasis sweep.** `model-mono-emphasis-sweep.wav`: one held note,
      the cutoff swept by hand while the emphasis rises from none to 95%.
      Pass: the resonance thickens and then whistles over the note, and
      the bass drops away as the emphasis rises, as the circuit's does
      (the toolkit puts 30% of it back).
- [ ] **Oscillator 3 as a modulator.** `model-mono-osc3-modulation.wav`:
      the lead with oscillator 3 at 32' following the keys, the wheel
      pushed up over the note into both the pitch and the filter. Pass:
      it goes from a plain lead through a growl to a clangorous one.
- [ ] **Wind.** `model-mono-wind.wav`: pink noise through the ladder with
      a slow contour and oscillator 3 wobbling the cutoff. Pass: wind.
- [ ] **In the app.** Add a Model Mono track (Ctrl+K, "Add Model Mono
      track"), choose **Fat bass** under **Sound**, play. Pass: it sounds
      like the render; the mod wheel adds nothing until **Mod to pitch**
      or **Mod to filter** is on; the controls are in groups and each is
      reachable by an assistant (`instrument.cutoff` and the rest).

## Decisions

- [ ] **Default note priority.** The classic plays the lowest key; the
      synth defaults to the last key played, which overlapping notes in
      a clip handle better. Keep last, or follow the classic?
- [ ] **The release switch's default.** On (the release takes the decay
      time), so notes from a clip don't click off; the classic's
      default position is off (a quick release). Keep on?
- [ ] **Full-wheel depth.** Pitch modulation at full wheel is a fifth
      either way, filter modulation three octaves. Enough, too much?

## Measured

One voice at 48 kHz in a release build on the cloud box, with the
mixer hot, oscillator 3 and noise in the mix and pitch modulation on:
about 1% of one core (`one_voice_costs_little`, release builds only).
The golden render test `a_fixed_patch_renders_to_its_known_spectrum`
holds the fat bass at C2's harmonics to within 1.5 dB.

## Not built

- The external input (running an instrument through the filter) is
  left to the semi-modular synth (S7), whose patch points it fits.
- A pitch-bend wheel range and the glide switch are settings rather
  than the classic's performance switches; there is no "A-440" tone.
