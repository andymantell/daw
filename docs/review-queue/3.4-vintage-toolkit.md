# 3.4 Vintage synth toolkit (classic synths, step 0)

The shared parts the classic-synth instruments are built on
(`crates/instruments/src/vintage/`, plan `docs/plans/classic-synths.md`):
five saturating filters run at twice the sample rate, drifting
band-limited oscillators with sync and sub-oscillators, analogue-curve
envelopes, a bucket-brigade chorus and shared voice handling. The
existing synth ("Synth" in the instrument list, `poly-synth` to
assistants) gained the filters as choices under **Filter** (Ladder 24 dB,
Diode ladder, Cascade 24 dB, Cascade 12 dB, Sallen-Key), a **Drive**
control and a **Vintage drift** control, so the toolkit can be heard
in the app before the synths built on it land.

## Listening

The renders are in the project files under `synths/listening/`
(`cargo run -p daw-instruments --release --example vintage_listen -- <dir>`
makes them again). Each is normalised to the same peak.

- [ ] **Bass sweep, filter by filter.** `bass-sweep-<filter>.wav`: the
      same saw-and-sub bass line with a quick filter decay and resonance
      at 60–75% through each filter, and `bass-sweep-linear-old.wav`
      through the old clean filter for comparison. Pass: the vintage
      ones have weight and a resonance that sings rather than a thin
      "wah"; the ladder and cascade are the ones to compare for a Moog-
      or Juno-style bass, the diode for acid. Say which, if any, still
      sounds basic, and whether the diode ladder's dull passband (its
      corner sits about three octaves under where it resonates, see the
      table) is right or should be brightened.
- [ ] **Self-oscillation.** `self-oscillation-<filter>.wav`: no
      oscillator, resonance full, the cutoff played as a melody with
      glide. Pass: a clean sine-like whistle that follows the melody on
      the ladder, cascade and diode; the Sallen-Key one is meant to be
      louder and rougher (it screams past its threshold). Say if any is
      too quiet to be useful: the level each settles at is set by its
      clipping, and is easy to raise.
- [ ] **Overdriven mixer.** `overdriven-mixer.wav`: two saws and a pulse,
      a fifth apart with the bass an octave down, the drive rising from
      clean to hard over eight seconds into the ladder. Pass: it
      thickens and growls as it goes without fizz or aliasing whistles
      at the top.
- [ ] **Slow pad with drift and chorus.** `slow-pad-chorus.wav`: six
      voices of two drifting oscillators each, pulse width moving, the
      cascade filter opening over six seconds, stereo chorus on both
      sweeps. Pass: it moves and shimmers on its own; the drift sounds
      like an old instrument, not out of tune (it is ±4 cents fixed and
      ±3 cents wandering per voice at full "vintage").
- [ ] **Acid line.** `acid-line-diode.wav`: a square wave through the
      diode ladder with accents that pile up the sweep and slides between
      overlapping notes, the second pass brighter. Pass: it squelches.
- [ ] **In the app.** Add a Synth track, set Filter to Ladder 24 dB,
      Drive to 50% and Vintage drift to 50%, play a bass. Pass: it sounds
      like the renders, and the controls are in Ctrl+K and reachable by
      an assistant (`instrument.filter`, `instrument.drive`,
      `instrument.vintage`).

## Measured response

At a 1 kHz cutoff, a 5% full-scale sine, dB at each frequency, with no
resonance and then with resonance at 70%:

| filter | 50 Hz | 500 | 1 k | 2 k | 4 k | 8 k |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ladder, res 0 | 0.0 | −4.0 | −12.3 | −28.4 | −49.6 | −72.6 |
| ladder, res 0.7 | −6.7 | −4.2 | +5.7 | −22.1 | −43.7 | −66.7 |
| diode, res 0 | −1.1 | −16.3 | −26.0 | −40.0 | −58.8 | −81.5 |
| diode, res 0.7 | −10.6 | −11.8 | +0.4 | −28.3 | −48.0 | −70.6 |
| cascade 24 dB, res 0 | −0.1 | −3.9 | −12.1 | −28.0 | −49.4 | −73.3 |
| cascade 24 dB, res 0.7 | −6.7 | −4.3 | +5.9 | −21.7 | −43.4 | −67.3 |
| state-variable, res 0 | 0.0 | −1.9 | −6.0 | −14.0 | −24.7 | −36.7 |
| state-variable, res 0.7 | 0.0 | +1.8 | +4.4 | −10.2 | −23.7 | −36.4 |
| Sallen-Key, res 0 | 0.0 | −1.9 | −6.0 | −14.0 | −24.7 | −36.7 |
| Sallen-Key, res 0.7 | 0.0 | +2.2 | +8.0 | −9.9 | −23.7 | −36.4 |
| old clean filter, res 0 | 0.0 | −1.9 | −6.0 | −14.0 | −25.0 | −37.9 |

What the published descriptions say, and what this shows:

- Four coincident poles are 12 dB down at the cutoff and fall 24 dB an
  octave above it: the ladder and cascade do (−12.3 and −12.1 at 1 kHz,
  −21 to −24 dB between 4 and 8 kHz). Resonance peaks at the cutoff and,
  on the ladder and cascade as in the circuits, takes the bass with it
  (−6.7 dB at 50 Hz at 70%); each has a compensation setting for the
  synths that want the bass kept.
- The diode ladder's stages load each other, which spreads its poles:
  its slope starts gently (18 dB an octave over the first octaves, the
  figure it is often mislabelled with) and reaches 24 dB only well above
  the cutoff. Its resonance point is where "cutoff" is defined, so its
  passband corner sits far below it. The high-pass in its feedback keeps
  the lowest bass (−1 dB at 50 Hz with no resonance) while thinning the
  mids as resonance rises.
- The two-pole filters are 6 dB down at the cutoff and fall 12 dB an
  octave, and their resonance leaves the bass alone.
- Each resonance lands within 3% of the set cutoff from 100 Hz to 5 kHz
  (tests check it) and each filter self-oscillates at the top of its
  resonance range.

## CPU

Measured in a release build on the cloud test machine (slower than a
desktop), one oversampled filter per voice, per sample: ladder 135 ns,
cascade 200 ns, diode 250 ns, the oversampling itself 33 ns. Sixteen
voices of ladder are about 10% of one core here at 48 kHz, about half
that on a current desktop. The synth threads measure their own budget;
if it needs to come down, running voices in lockstep (SIMD) is the next
step, and is noted in the plan.

## Decisions to accept

- [ ] **The diode ladder's coupling.** Built as a fully unbuffered
      ladder (every stage loaded by the next), which is what gives it
      the gentle knee and the 18 dB-looking slope. A half-coupled model
      is brighter with a sharper peak. Listen to the acid line and say
      if it should be brighter.
- [ ] **Self-oscillation levels.** The ladder, cascade and diode settle
      at −15 to −20 dB when singing on their own; the Sallen-Key pair at
      about −2 dB. Keep, or make them alike?
