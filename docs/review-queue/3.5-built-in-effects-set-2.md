# 3.5 Built-in effects, set 2

Same `daw.exe`. Eight more effects in **Add effect…** under "Built in",
saved and undone like the first set. Built at risk while the owner was
away; nothing here has been listened to yet.

## On the PC

- [ ] **3.5 Picking one.** Click **FX** on a track and open **Add
      effect…**. Pass: the "Built in" group now also lists De-esser,
      Multiband compressor, Transient shaper, Saturation, Chorus,
      Flanger, Phaser and Tremolo, and hovering each says what it does.
- [ ] **3.5 Saved and reopened.** Add a few of the new effects, change
      settings, save, close and reopen. Pass: same effects, same
      settings, same sound. Undo after a change puts it back.
- [ ] **3.5 No glitches.** While a backing track plays, drag each new
      effect's sliders and switch its choices (Voices, Stages, Shape,
      Character). Pass: no crackles beyond the change itself.
      Settings → Performance shows no overloads. Saturation is the
      heaviest of the new ones (about 5% of one core in the cloud, near
      the guitar amp's).

## Listening

- [ ] **3.5 Chorus.** On a clean guitar. Pass: thicker and wider, with no
      seasick wobble at the default Speed and Depth; 1, 2 and 3 Voices
      each sound fuller than the last.
- [ ] **3.5 Flanger.** On a strummed guitar or drums. Pass: a smooth
      sweep; high or negative Feedback makes it ring metallically, with
      no clicks at the sweep's ends.
- [ ] **3.5 Phaser.** Try 2, 4 and 12 Stages and some Feedback. Pass: a
      smooth swirl; more stages give more notches.
- [ ] **3.5 Tremolo.** Try each Shape and Side to side. Pass: an even
      pulse; Square has no clicks. Say if you want its Speed to follow
      the project tempo (not built yet).
- [ ] **3.5 Saturation.** On a bass, a vocal and a drum bus, each
      Character, 1 to 4 Stages, Drive low to high. Pass: turning Drive up
      adds colour more than loudness, more Stages at the same Drive is
      smoother, and high notes don't whistle. Say which Character sounds
      best and whether a dry/wet Mix is wanted (left out because the
      effect containers in A4 will give a parallel mix to any effect).
- [ ] **3.5 Transient shaper.** On drums or a picked acoustic. Pass:
      Attack up makes hits snappier, down softens them; Sustain up brings
      the ring and room up, down tightens it. Try Attack length short and
      long.
- [ ] **3.5 De-esser.** On a vocal with sharp "s" sounds (or a bright
      pick attack). Turn on **Hear what it listens to**, move Frequency
      until you hear mostly the "s", turn it off, then lower Threshold
      until "Turning down" reads a few dB on each "s". Pass: the "s"
      softens without a lisp, in both **Only the highs** and **The whole
      sound**.
- [ ] **3.5 Multiband compressor.** On a full mix or a bass. Pass: with
      every Ratio at 1:1 and every level at 0 dB it sounds the same as
      off; each band's threshold and ratio tame only its own range;
      moving the splits while playing doesn't click.
