# 3.5 Effect containers

Same `daw.exe`. Built at risk while the owner was away; nothing here
has been tried with real plugins or a guitar yet. Engine and splitter
behaviour is covered by tests (`crates/engine/tests/containers.rs`,
`crates/effects/src/container.rs`).

## What was built

Four containers, in a new **Containers** group of **Add effect…**:

- **Parallel chains**: one to four chains, each fed a copy of the
  sound, added up with the untouched (dry) sound. Each chain has its
  own level, and the dry sound has its own level, off by default.
- **Split by frequency**: two to four bands with movable crossovers.
  The bands add back up to the original level at every frequency
  (within 0.05 dB in the tests), and each band has a level.
- **Mid/side split**: one chain for what's in the middle, one for the
  sides, each with a level (turning Side off makes the sound mono).
- **Left/right split**: one chain per side.

Under a container, the effects window shows each chain with its own
**Add…** list (built-in effects, containers and plugins). An effect's
**Move** menu puts it into any chain, or back on the track's own chain.
▲ and ▼ move it within its chain. Removing a container removes what's
inside it. Containers can hold containers. Each chain is delayed to
match the slowest one, so a plugin with latency in one chain stays in
time with the others and with the dry sound.

## Decisions taken while you were away

- [ ] **Parallel chains are added, not averaged.** Two empty chains
      play the sound twice as loud (+6 dB), as two mixer channels would.
      The chain levels are there to bring it down. Pass: you're happy
      with that, or say if you'd rather it started each chain lower.
- [ ] **The dry sound starts off.** For parallel compression, turn
      **Dry level** up to 0 dB and put a compressor in Chain 1.
- [ ] **Mid/side and left/right chains run in mono.** Each chain gets
      its half of the sound on both sides, and its output is the average
      of the two. Stereo effects (a stereo chorus, say) collapse to mono
      inside them.
- [ ] **Fewer chains keeps their effects.** Turning **Chains** or
      **Bands** down leaves the effects in the chains that went, marked
      "not playing", until they're moved or the chain comes back.

## On the PC

- [ ] **3.5 Parallel compression.** On a guitar or drum track add
      **Parallel chains**, set **Chains** to 1, **Dry level** to 0 dB,
      and add a compressor to Chain 1 with heavy settings. Pass: the
      sound keeps its attack with the squashed sound underneath, and no
      flanging or comb-filter sound (they're lined up).
- [ ] **3.5 A plugin with latency in one chain.** Put a plugin that
      reports latency (a linear-phase EQ, a look-ahead limiter) in one
      parallel chain and leave the other empty. Pass: no flanging, and
      the track stays in time with the others.
- [ ] **3.5 Splitting by frequency.** Add **Split by frequency** with 3
      bands and nothing in them. Pass: it sounds the same as without.
      Then put a distortion on the middle band only. Pass: the bass and
      the top stay clean.
- [ ] **3.5 Mid/side.** On a stereo mix add **Mid/side split** and turn
      **Side level** down. Pass: it narrows towards mono; turned up, it
      widens.
- [ ] **3.5 Saving and undo.** Save, reopen, and undo moving an effect
      between chains. Pass: everything comes back where it was.
- [ ] **3.5 Performance.** A container with four chains of built-in
      effects. Pass: Settings → Performance shows no overloads.
