# 3.6 Content browser

Screenshots, from the cloud, in `/mnt/project-files/screenshots/`:
`app-browser.png` (a folder of sounds, tags, one loop playing at the
project's tempo, its details below), `app-browser-search.png` (searching

project's own audio). The small icons (▶, ■, the folder) show as boxes
in the cloud, as on other screenshots.

Built at risk: previews at the project's tempo use our own stretcher,
which still waits on the speed and pitch choice (0.6).

## On the PC

- [ ] **Sound folders.** View → Content browser (Ctrl+B). Press Add
      folder… and choose a folder of samples or loops (one with
      subfolders). Pass: its sounds are listed within a few seconds, and
      after a restart the folder is still there. Add a file to the folder
      and press Look again: it appears.
- [ ] **Hearing sounds.** Click a sound's name (Play on click is on) or
      its ▶. Click another while it plays, then ■. Pass: each starts at
      once (a second or less), the new one replaces the old, nothing
      clicks or crackles, and it is heard through the speakers chosen in
      the monitor section. Try it while the song plays too.
- [ ] **At the project's tempo.** Pick a loop with its tempo in its name
      (such as "groove 96bpm"), set the project to a different tempo, and
      play it with At project tempo on, then off. Pass: on, it keeps time
      with the metronome and keeps its pitch; the line under the list
      says the speed. For a loop with no tempo in its name, check the
      guess shown (~ before it) and set the right one in Tempo if wrong.
- [ ] **Tags and search.** Give a few sounds tags (type "drums, funk" in
      Tags and press Enter). Search for a word in a name, then click a tag.
      Pass: the list narrows as you type; tags stay after a restart.
- [ ] **Adding to the project.** Put the playhead at bar 3 and press Add
      to project (or double-click a name). Pass: a new track with the
      sound starts at the playhead. It is then listed under This project.
