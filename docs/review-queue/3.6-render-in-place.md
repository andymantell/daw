# 3.6 Freeze, render in place, bounce and paste as rendered audio

Same `daw.exe`. Screenshots from the cloud:
`/mnt/project-files/screenshots/app-render-track-menu.png` (a track's menu
on the timeline) and `app-render-in-place.png` (a guitar with a delay
rendered in place, and the guitar pasted onto the bass as rendered audio).
In the cloud, each render played back matched the original through its
effects to within 1/10 000 of full scale. Built at risk with no listening.

Renders are made before the fader, so the new track keeps the original's
volume, pan, output, sends and control group, and sounds the same. Renders
of tracks with effects start a second early (hidden, for the effects'
delay) and run two seconds past the last clip, for tails.

- [ ] **Render in place.** Right-click an empty part of a track with an
      amp sim or delay on it and choose Render in place. Pass: a progress
      line shows in the status bar, then a new track "… (rendered)"
      appears under it with one clip, the original is muted, and playing
      sounds the same as before. Ctrl+Z puts it back as it was in one
      step.
- [ ] **Render a group in place.** Put two tracks in a folder (or route
      them to a bus) with an effect on the folder, and choose Render group
      in place on the folder. Pass: the new track sits after the folder's
      tracks, outside it, the folder is muted, and it sounds the same.
      Tracks that send to the bus (a reverb return) are included.
- [ ] **Bounce.** Select a few clips on one track, right-click one and
      choose Bounce to a new track (also in the Edit menu). Pass: a
      "… (bounced)" track with just those clips' audio, through the
      track's effects; the clips are muted where they were.
- [ ] **Copy and paste.** Select clips, Ctrl+C, move the playhead, Ctrl+V.
      Pass: copies land at the playhead on their own tracks, keeping their
      spacing, and are selected. Right-click another track and choose
      Paste here: they land there instead.
- [ ] **Paste as rendered audio.** Copy a clip from a track with effects,
      right-click another track and choose Paste here as rendered audio.
      Pass: one clip "… (rendered)" arrives there sounding as the clip did
      through its own track's effects.
- [ ] **Freeze.** In a saved project, choose Freeze on a track with
      plugins. Pass: its effects window says it is frozen, the mixer's FX
      button shows ❄, it sounds the same, and the performance check shows
      less load with heavy plugins. Unfreeze brings the effects back.
      In an unsaved project it says it freezes once saved.
- [ ] **Decide:** whether freezing should also unload the plugins (it
      only switches them off now, so they still hold memory), and whether
      editing a frozen track's clips should be blocked (now it just plays
      through its effects again until the next save freezes it again).
- [ ] **Automation.** Draw a volume lane and a lane for a built-in
      effect's setting on a track, then render it in place. Pass: it
      sounds the same; the effect's lane is in the audio, and the volume
      (and pan and send) lanes move to the new track, as its fader is
      after the render. Plugin parameter lanes aren't rendered yet, as
      they aren't exported either.
