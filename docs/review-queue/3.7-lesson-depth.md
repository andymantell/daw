# 3.7 Lesson depth: practice log, lessons, assignments

Practice → Lessons opens a pane with three tabs: Assignments, Practice log
and History. Screenshots (drawn by the test harness, whose fonts lack a few
icons): `app-lessons-assignments.png`, `app-lessons-set.png`,
`app-lessons-practice-log.png` and `app-lessons-history.png`.

## Checks on the PC

- [ ] **3.7 Practice is logged as you play.** Open a song with markers.
      Loop a section, slow it to 70 %, turn the trainer on, and play for a
      minute or two. Stop. Then loop another section and play again.
      Open Practice → Lessons → Practice log. Pass: one session per
      stretch of playing, with the section's name, the time, the times
      round the loop and the top speed and tempo the trainer reached.
      Stretches under 10 seconds are left out. Playing without the loop
      shows as "Whole song". Save, close and reopen: the log is still
      there.
- [ ] **3.7 Setting and practising an assignment.** Under Assignments,
      press Set an assignment. Pick a section, a tempo and a due date, add
      a note, and press Set. Press Practise. Pass: the section loops from
      its start, with the trainer on and aiming at the tempo. After
      playing it, the assignment shows your best tempo for all its bars,
      the time spent and the loops. Practising only part of its bars
      adds time but not tempo. Change and Delete work, and Edit → Undo
      brings a deleted one back.
- [ ] **3.7 Lessons.** Under History, press Start a lesson. Pass: it is
      listed as Lesson 1 with today's date, a snapshot of the same name
      appears under File → Snapshots…, and assignments set after it say
      "set in Lesson 1". Play for a while: the strip at the top shows a
      bar for today, and the lesson shows the practice after it. What
      changed since… lists what has been done to the song since. Rename
      and Remove work; removing keeps the snapshot.
- [ ] **3.7 Teacher and student see the same progress.** With the
      teacher's copy in the shared folder (or a bundle each way), the
      teacher sets an assignment, the student practises it and presses
      Mark done, then each opens the other's changes. Pass: both see the
      student's progress and "Done" with the date, under the student's
      name. A teacher who is a commenter can't set assignments or start
      lessons, but their own practice and Mark done do get through.

## Decisions

- [ ] **Dates are shown in UTC.** The app has no time zone table, so a
      session at 00:30 in summer time shows the day before. Say if that
      matters; a time zone library would fix it.
- [ ] **What counts towards an assignment.** Time counts for any loop
      that takes in some of its bars; tempo counts only for a loop that
      takes in all of them, at the tempo where the loop starts. Playing
      without the loop counts towards no assignment. Say if the teacher
      would rather count it differently.
- [ ] **Who may set assignments.** Owners and editors set assignments and
      start lessons; anyone in the project keeps their own log and marks
      their own assignments done. If the teacher is to be a commenter,
      say so and assignments could move out of the song to where any
      member may write them.
