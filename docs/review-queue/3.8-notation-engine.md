# 3.8 Notation engine (tab, staff and drum layout, MusicXML)

The new `notation` crate, built at risk on decision 0009, which is still
proposed. It is a library only: nothing in the app shows it yet (that is
the notation lanes item, B5 in the Phase 3 plan). Engraved pages from the
cloud: `/mnt/project-files/notation/engraved-page-1.png` to `-3.png`, the
same pages as a PDF (`engraved-demo.pdf`), the plain-text tab
(`demo-tab.txt`) and the MusicXML (`demo.musicxml`). Make them again with
`cargo run -p daw-notation --example engrave`.

## Reading the engraved pages

- [ ] **3.8 Tab reads like tab.** Open the PNGs or the PDF. Pass: fret
      numbers sit on their strings with the line cleared behind them,
      chords stack in one column, the strings and the TAB sign line up,
      and the rhythm stems under the tab match the notes above.
- [ ] **3.8 Techniques.** Pass: you recognise each mark: hammer-on (H)
      and pull-off (P) arcs, slides, the bend arrow with "full" and its
      release, the vibrato wiggle, P.M. and "let ring" with dashed lines,
      the dead note (x), the natural harmonic (<12> with N.H.), tapping
      (T), slap (S) and pop (P) on the bass, tremolo picking (the slashes
      on the stem), the ghost note in brackets, and the strum arrow
      before the G chord.
- [ ] **3.8 Standard staff above the tab.** Pass: the notes are where you
      would expect them in the octave-down treble clef, with ledger lines,
      the G major key signature, beams within each beat, the triplet "3",
      and dots after dotted notes.
- [ ] **3.8 Drum staff.** Pass: hi-hats above the staff with x heads,
      snare on the third space, bass drum on the bottom space with stems
      down (feet) and hands stems up, the open hi-hat "o", accents, ghost
      notes in brackets, the flam and drag grace notes, the roll slashes,
      the cymbal choke mark and the side-stick circled x, and a legend of
      every kit piece used.
- [ ] **3.8 Printing.** Print `engraved-demo.pdf` on A4. Pass: it prints
      cleanly at full size, three pages, page numbers at the bottom.

## Trying the files elsewhere

- [ ] **3.8 MusicXML out.** Open `demo.musicxml` in any notation program
      you have. Pass: the guitar part opens as tab (with the staff above
      it), the bass as four-string tab, the drums on a percussion staff,
      and the notes, rhythms and chord names match the PNGs. Techniques
      with no MusicXML element (palm mute, let ring, slap, pop, choke,
      rimshot, cross-stick) are written as named "other technical" marks,
      which other programs may show as text or not at all.
- [ ] **3.8 MusicXML in.** Export a tab and a drum part you already have
      to MusicXML from another program and send the files over. Pass: the
      crate's tests read them (we'll add them as test files).
- [ ] **3.8 Text tab.** Paste a few lines of `demo-tab.txt` into a
      message. Pass: it reads like the tab people post online.

## Decisions to accept

- [ ] **Decision 0009 (notation formats).** Everything here follows it:
      MusicXML 4.0 as the main format, plain-text tab, print/PDF, our own
      layout with a music font in the standard music-font layout, and no
      proprietary tab-editor formats. Accepting it takes the "at risk"
      off this work.
- [ ] **The bundled music font.** The reference font of the standard
      music-font layout, under the SIL Open Font License, is in
      `crates/app/fonts/bravura/` with its licence. Bundling it is allowed;
      say if you'd rather we used another.
- [ ] **Drum map places.** The standard kit map puts toms, cymbals and
      hi-hats on the places in `crates/notation/src/drums.rs` (for
      example low floor tom on the second space up, high tom on the top
      line, crash on the ledger line above). Conventions vary for toms;
      say if your teacher's charts put them elsewhere.
- [ ] **Strum arrows.** Built: a downstroke is an arrow pointing down
      the page, the same way as the standard music font's downstroke
      sign, and is written to MusicXML as an arpeggio going up in pitch
      (low strings first). Say if you read strum arrows the other way.
- [ ] **Automatic string and fret choice.** Notes without positions get
      the hand shape that moves least, preferring lower positions and
      keeping hammer-ons, pull-offs and slides on one string. Locked notes
      and locked hand positions are kept. Pass: the guitar part's choices
      in the demo look like what you would play.
