# 3.8 Notation lanes (tab, staff and drum staff on the timeline)

Built at risk while the owner was away (plan item B5), on decision 0009,
which is still proposed. An instrument track can show its notes as tab,
a standard staff or a drum staff in a band under its row on the
timeline. The band is a view of the track's notes, not a separate
score: the note editor, the band and the instrument all use the same
notes. Every action is a command (the new **Notation** menu, and
Ctrl+K), so keys and anything driving the app from outside reach the
same thing.

Screenshots (drawn by the test harness, whose fonts lack a few icons):
`/mnt/project-files/screenshots/app-notation-lanes.png` (guitar tab with

## On the PC

- [ ] **3.8 Show a lane.** Add an instrument track, draw a few notes in
      the note editor, then Notation → Show notation lane. Pass: tab
      appears under the track's row, each fret number under where its
      note plays on the timeline, and stays lined up as you zoom and
      scroll. Zoomed far out, the band says to zoom in instead of
      drawing a jumble.
- [ ] **3.8 Follows the song.** Add a tempo change and a 3/4 bar
      (Changes…). Pass: the band's bar lines stay on the ruler's bars,
      and notes stay under where they play.
- [ ] **3.8 Scrolling with playback.** Play. Pass: the lanes turn a page
      when the playhead reaches the edge, as before. Notation → Scroll
      along with playback: the lanes now move under a still playhead.
      Say which you'd rather have by default when a notation lane shows.
- [ ] **3.8 Typing tab.** Press T (Type tab), or click on the tab. Pass:
      a highlighted column and a box on a string show where the next
      number goes. Numbers write frets (type 1 then 2 quickly for 12),
      Left/Right move along by the step, Up/Down change string, `,` and
      `.` halve and double the step, Delete clears, Escape stops. The
      notes play what the tab says (tuning and capo included).
- [ ] **3.8 Strings from the note editor.** Select notes in the note
      editor, then Notation → Move notes to the string above / below
      (or Shift+Up / Shift+Down while typing tab). Pass: the same notes
      move to another string with the right frets.
- [ ] **3.8 Tuning, capo, staff.** Notation → Notation lane…: try Drop
      D, a capo, the staff above the tab, and rhythm off. Pass: the
      band redraws each time and the frets follow the capo.
- [ ] **3.8 Drums.** Import a drum MIDI file (on channel 10). Pass: it
      comes in with a drum staff, hi-hats on top with x heads, hands
      stems up and feet stems down.
- [ ] **3.8 MusicXML in.** Import MusicXML… with a tab or drum part you
      have (exported from another program). Pass: a track per part,
      shown as its tab or drum staff, with the score's tempo and time
      signatures when the project was empty.
- [ ] **3.8 Files out.** Export notation as MusicXML…, Export tab as
      text…, Print notation to PDF…. Pass: the files open elsewhere and
      match the lanes.
- [ ] **3.8 Practice.** Notation → Practice-mute this part, then play
      along with the count-in on. Pass: the part goes quiet (the mixer's
      headphones button on the track lights, and the band says "muted to
      play"), and the count-in counts the bar the notation shows.

## Decisions to accept

- [ ] **Decision 0009 (notation formats)** is still proposed. This work
      follows it, as the notation engine did; accepting it takes the
      "at risk" off both.
- [ ] **How notes are written.** The band moves notes to the nearest
      sixteenth, or to triplet eighths where a beat's notes fit those
      better, holds each note until the next one starts, and splits a
      length at the next beat when it starts off the beat. Notes that
      overlap are cut where the next starts. Say if a part you
      transcribe reads wrongly.
- [ ] **Fret numbers are counted from the capo**, as players read them,
      in the notes too. A note keeps the string and fret it was typed
      on; notes without one show the assigner's choice until "Choose
      strings and frets" writes it into them.
- [ ] **Clicking the tab starts typing.** A click on a tab band puts the
      cursor there straight away; a click anywhere else on the lanes
      stops. Say if you'd rather it took a key or a double-click.

## Not built yet

- Editing the drum map per project, and its printed legend on the
  lane (the PDF has the legend). The lane uses the standard percussion
  key map.
- Chord names and boxes on the lane (the engine draws them; notes have
  nowhere to keep them yet), and hand-position locks.
- Plain-text tab import with a review step (roadmap 3.8): only text tab
  export is built.
- Tab entry from a MIDI guitar controller (plan Set C).
