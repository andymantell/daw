# 4.A4 Song sections, and inserting and deleting bars

Built at risk (Phase 4 plan item A4). Inserting and deleting bars
landed first (PR #84), as writing a play order into the song uses it.
Screenshots from the cloud, in `/mnt/project-files/screenshots/`:
`app-sections.png`, `app-sections-written.png` and
`app-insert-delete-bars.png`.

## On the PC

- [ ] **4.A4 Insert bars.** In a song with audio, notes, a tempo
      change, markers and a volume line, set the loop over two bars in
      the middle and choose Edit → Insert bars at the loop (or "insert"
      in the command search). Pass: two empty bars appear at the loop's
      start; every track's audio and notes after them, the markers, the
      tempo change and the volume line move two bars later and still
      line up; a clip across the point is cut in two, and plays the same
      as before either side of the gap; the volume line holds level over
      the gap. Undo puts it all back in one step.
- [ ] **4.A4 Delete bars.** Set the loop over two bars and choose
      Edit → Delete the loop's bars. Pass: what was in them is gone from
      every track, the rest of the song closes up and plays in time, and
      the tempo and time signature after the cut are as they were.
      Comments in the cut move to it rather than going.
- [ ] **4.A4 Any bars.** Edit → Insert or delete bars… Pass: it opens
      with the loop's bars filled in; changing "From bar" and "Bars" and
      pressing either button does the same from there.
- [ ] **4.A4 Practise a section on a loop.** A section runs from one
      marker to the next (the last to the end of the song). Mark a few
      with M, put the playhead in one and choose Edit → Practise
      this section (or Loop in Edit → Sections…).
      Pass: the loop covers exactly that section's bars and plays round
      it. Try it in a lesson.
- [ ] **4.A4 Sections window.** Edit → Sections…
      Screenshot: `app-sections.png`. Pass: each section is listed with
      its bars; "(here)" marks the playhead's; Earlier / Later swap a
      section with its neighbour on every track, with notes, audio,
      automation and tempo going along; Copy after puts a copy straight
      after it; Delete takes its bars out. Each is one undo step.
- [ ] **4.A4 Play order.** In the window, press "+ Play order" on
      sections in any order, some more than once, then "Play in this
      order". Pass: the song plays each section in turn and goes from
      one to the next with no gap (the change happens at the exact
      sample), going round the whole order until you stop; your own loop
      is back when you stop. The order is saved with the project.
      Clicking a section in the order takes it out.
- [ ] **4.A4 Write the order into the song.** Press "Write into the
      song". Screenshot: `app-sections-written.png`. Pass: the timeline
      is laid out in that order, with a copy of each repeated section
      and its marker; a section's tab and comments stay with its first
      time; a video and its hit points stay where they were. Undo puts
      the song back.
