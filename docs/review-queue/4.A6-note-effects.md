# Phase 4 A6: Note effects

Built at risk while the owner was away. An instrument track's notes can
now go through note effects before they reach the instrument: an
arpeggiator, chord, note length, velocity, randomise and scale. They
sit at the head of the track's chain, so notes from clips and notes
played live on a keyboard both go through them, in the order listed,
and the track's MIDI output gets what they make. They are in the
instrument window (Notes → Instrument…), under "Note effects", and in
the Notes menu (Add note effect, Bypass note effects, Render note
effects into the notes, Remove note effects). Screenshot (drawn by the
test harness, whose fonts lack the arrow and cross icons):
`/mnt/project-files/screenshots/app-note-effects.png`.

What each does:

- **Arpeggiator**: plays the keys held one after another. Order: up,
  down, up and down, down and up, as played, random, or chord (all
  held notes on each step). Rate from 1/4 to 1/32 with triplets,
  1 to 4 octaves, gate (how long each step sounds), latch, and a fixed
  velocity or as played. While the song plays, steps fall on the
  song's grid; when stopped they start from the first key.
- **Chord**: a chord on every note. Fixed shapes (major, minor, power,
  octave, sus2, sus4, major 7, minor 7, dominant 7) or a triad or
  seventh built from the song's key.
- **Note length**: notes exactly a length, at least a length, or
  longer by a length.
- **Velocity**: scale, add, softest and hardest.
- **Randomise**: pitch and velocity moved a little at random, a chance
  of each note playing, optionally kept in the key.
- **Scale**: notes off the song's key moved to the nearest, up, down,
  or left out.

The key is the song's key from the note editor. With none set, the
effects that use one follow C major, and the window says so.

## On the PC

- [ ] **A6 Playing a keyboard through the arpeggiator.** Arm or monitor
      an instrument track, add Notes → Add note effect → Arpeggiator,
      and hold a chord. Pass: the notes play one after another at
      sixteenths; letting go stops them; with Latch on they carry on
      until new keys are pressed. Pressing play, the steps line up with
      the metronome.
- [ ] **A6 Changing settings while holding keys.** Hold a chord and
      change the rate, order or octaves. Pass: the change is heard on
      the next step, with no stuck notes and no gap.
- [ ] **A6 Chord and scale on a keyboard.** Set a key in the note
      editor, add Chord (Triad in the key) and play single notes.
      Pass: each plays a chord in the key; letting go stops all of it.
      Add Scale (Leave out) above it and play black keys in C major:
      they are silent.
- [ ] **A6 Rendering.** On a track with a note clip, add effects and
      choose Render into notes. Pass: the clip now shows the notes the
      effects played, it sounds the same, and the effects are switched
      off (ticked off in the window). Undo puts it back.
- [ ] **A6 Saving.** Save, close and open the project. Pass: the
      effects and their settings come back.

## Decisions for the owner

- **Rendering makes plain notes.** Rendered notes keep pitch, velocity,
  channel, start and length; a note's chance, per-note expression,
  string and fret are not carried over. Fine, or should notes that an
  effect only changes (velocity, scale) keep them?
- **Rendering acts on every note clip on the track**, and turns the
  effects off for the whole track, so nothing plays twice. Pattern
  clips become plain note clips. Should it be per clip instead?
- **First step when stopped.** With the transport stopped, the first
  key plays at once and later steps follow from it. While playing, the
  first key also plays at once and the next step waits for the grid.
  Some arpeggiators wait for the grid even for the first step.
- **No key set** means C major for the key-based effects. Alternative:
  have them do nothing until a key is set.

## For an assistant

Every action is a command (`add-arpeggiator`, `add-chord-effect`,
`add-note-length-effect`, `add-velocity-effect`, `add-randomise-effect`,
`add-scale-effect`, `note-effects-on-off`, `render-note-effects`,
`remove-note-effects`). `get_project` lists each track's note effects,
and `list_parameters` / `set_parameter` read and set their settings as
`notefx:<effect id>:<setting>` (and `notefx:<effect id>:on`), with the
choices a setting takes.
