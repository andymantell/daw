# 4 (A8) Swapping projects with other DAWs

File → **Export for other DAWs…** writes a `.dawproject` file (the open
DAWproject 1.0 exchange format), after a window listing what won't go.
File → **Import from another DAW…** opens one as a new, unsaved project
and lists what didn't come in. Assistants can do both through the agent
interface (`export_report`, `export_project_file`, `import_project_file`).
The reader and writer are our own, in `crates/interchange`, written from
the format's published schema and reference.

Checked in the cloud: our own projects go out and come back with tracks,
routing, sends, clips (to the frame), fades, notes with per-note
expression and controller lines, automation (to the frame), tempo and
time signature changes, markers, our effects and instruments with every
setting, and VST 3 and CLAP plugins with their settings. Hand-written
files laid out the way other DAWs write them (`crates/interchange/fixtures`)
come in with clips inside clips flattened, loops laid out pass by pass,
held automation steps kept as steps, and the generic EQ, compressor, gate
and limiter mapped to ours. No file from another DAW has been tried yet.

## On the PC

- [ ] **A song from another DAW.** Export a song with audio, an
      instrument part, a send to a reverb and some volume automation as a
      `.dawproject` from another DAW, and import it here. Pass: tracks,
      clips, notes, the send and the automation are where they were; the
      report lists anything else; plugins you have load with their
      settings. Send the file over if it may go in the test fixtures.
- [ ] **Our song into another DAW.** Export a lesson project here and open
      it in another DAW that reads the format. Pass: it opens; tracks,
      clips, notes, tempo and markers are in place; audio plays in time;
      plugins load with their settings.
- [ ] **CLAP plugin settings.** With a CLAP plugin on a track, export,
      then import into another DAW (or back here). Pass: the plugin opens
      with its settings. The format asks for a `.clap-preset` file but
      doesn't say what is in one; we write the plugin's saved state as it
      gives it, which is unconfirmed.

## Known gaps

- Audio stretched to fit the tempo in the other DAW plays at its own
  speed here, from where it started (said in the report), until clips can
  follow tempo (audio that follows tempo, A7).
- Clip gain and clip volume lines have no place in the format. Fade
  shapes go as lengths only.
- The export window suggests rendering a track to audio to keep its
  sound, but doesn't do it for you yet.
- Clip launcher scenes, video, master effects, note effects, and VST 2
  and AU plugins are left out of imports, and said so.
- The ratio of the generic compressor is a percentage in the format; we
  map 1:1 to 0%, 4:1 to 75% and ∞:1 to 100%. The format doesn't define
  the mapping.
- Per-note expression points are taken as counted from the note's start;
  the format's reference doesn't say.
