# 4.B4 Switching container (clean and driven amp channels)

Same `daw.exe`. Built at risk; nothing here has been tried with a guitar,
a real footswitch or a MIDI device yet. The sound side is covered by
tests in `crates/effects/src/container.rs`, and keys, footswitch,
automation and the preset by an end-to-end test driven through the
assistant interface (`crates/app/src/switch.rs`).

## What was built

- A new container, **Switch between chains**, in the **Containers**
  group of **Add effect…**. It has two to four chains and plays one at
  a time. Each chain has its own level, to match a quiet clean channel
  with a loud driven one.
- In the effects window, the container's row has a button per chain
  (**Chain 1**, **Chain 2**…); the one lit is heard. Each chain's row
  says **heard** or **not heard now**.
- **Keys:** **Edit → Switch to the next chain**, **Hear chain 1** and
  **Hear chain 2** (3 and 4 are in command search). They have no keys
  yet; give them one in **Settings → Key commands**. They act on the
  track whose effects window is open, or with a selected clip, or that
  is armed or monitoring, or else the first with a switching container.
- **Footswitch or MIDI:** in **Notes → MIDI learn…**, pick the track and
  **Switch between chains: Chain heard**, press **Learn** and press the
  footswitch. It is tied with **Each press steps** on, so each press
  moves to the next chain. Untick it for a latching switch or a pedal,
  where up is the last chain and down the first.
- **Automation:** the chain heard is a setting like any other, so it
  can have an automation lane (switch to the driven channel for the
  chorus).
- **Presets:** a chain preset keeps the container and both channels.
  One comes with the app: **Clean and driven guitar** (a compressor and
  clean amp, or a gate and crunch amp, then one reverb).

## Decisions taken while you were away

- [x] **Every chain keeps running, heard or not.** The one switched to
      is already warmed up (no thump from an amp starting from silence),
      at the cost of the processing for both channels. Pass: you're
      happy with that, or say if you'd rather the unheard chains rest.
- [x] **Switching fades across in about 30 ms.** Short enough to feel
      instant, long enough not to click. Echo and reverb tails inside
      the old chain are cut by the fade; put shared delay and reverb
      after the container (as the preset does) to keep them ringing.
- [x] **Switching is an undo step and is saved,** like moving any
      control: the project reopens on the chain last heard.
- [x] **Chains are named Chain 1, Chain 2…** There's no way to name
      them "Clean" and "Driven" yet. Say if you want that.
- [x] **A footswitch press is a controller going from below 64 to 64 or
      above.** Footswitches that send Program Change instead aren't
      understood yet.

## On the PC

- [ ] **B4 Clean and driven.** On a guitar track, add the preset
      **Clean and driven guitar**, monitor, and play. Press **Hear
      chain 2** from command search (Ctrl+K). Pass: it changes to the
      driven sound at once, with no click or gap, and back with **Hear
      chain 1**.
- [ ] **B4 A key.** Give **Switch to the next chain** a key in
      **Settings → Key commands** and switch while playing. Pass: it
      switches each press.
- [ ] **B4 A footswitch.** With a MIDI footswitch (or a MIDI keyboard's
      sustain pedal), tie it as above. Pass: each press switches
      channel, and holding it down doesn't switch again.
- [ ] **B4 Automation.** Add an automation lane for **Chain heard**,
      draw a step up at the chorus, and play from just before it and
      from the middle of it. Pass: it switches at the chorus, and
      starting in the middle plays the driven chain straight away.
- [ ] **B4 Levels.** Use **Chain 1 level** and **Chain 2 level** to
      even out the two channels. Pass: they're easy to match.
