# 4.D4 Fretboard view

Built while the owner was away (Phase 4 plan item D4). View → Fretboard
opens a pane under the timeline with a guitar or bass neck. It follows
the track the Notation commands act on (the note editor's clip, else the
first instrument track) and shows:

- the notes playing at the playhead, in green, on their own string and
  fret when the note has one (from tab), else at the lowest fret;
- the notes selected in the note editor, with a ring;
- a scale or mode (the song's key unless another is picked), one chord
  shape, or the chord's arpeggio, with the root in the accent colour,
  labelled with note names or steps from the root (1, b3, 5).

Clicking a fret enters that note at the playhead, a note-editor grid
step long, with its string and fret written in (so tab shows it there),
and plays it on the track's instrument ("Hear notes" turns that off).
While stopped, the playhead then moves on a step, so clicks write a
line. "Type on it" does the same from the keyboard: arrows move a box,
Enter enters, Escape stops. Notes held down on a MIDI keyboard or
guitar show on the neck in the record colour as they are played.

The tuning and capo are the track's tab lane's when it has one (changing
them here changes the lane, as the Notation lane window does); otherwise
the fretboard has its own. The hand position limits scales and
arpeggios to four frets and picks where the chord shape is found.

Every action is a command in the Notation → Fretboard menu and Ctrl+K,
so keys and assistants reach it, and assistants can read what it shows
with the `get_fretboard` tool.

Screenshots (drawn by the test harness, whose fonts lack a few icons):
`/mnt/project-files/screenshots/app-fretboard-scale.png` (A minor

## In a lesson

- [ ] **D4 Scales.** Set the song's key (Notes → Scale) to A minor, open
      View → Fretboard and pick "Scale or mode". Pass: the A minor
      notes cover the neck with every A marked as the root. Try minor
      pentatonic, blues, Dorian; set Position to 5 and only the box at
      the 5th fret shows.
- [ ] **D4 Chords.** Pick "Chord shape", then C at Position 1 and A
      minor at Position 5. Pass: the shapes are the ones you'd teach
      (x32010 and 577555). Say if other kinds of chord pick odd shapes;
      the shape finder is simple (root lowest, each string's nearest
      new chord note in the four frets).
- [ ] **D4 Playing along.** Play a track with tab (or import a guitar
      MIDI file). Pass: the green dots move with the music, on the
      strings the tab shows.
- [ ] **D4 Entering notes.** Stop, put the playhead at a bar, click a
      few frets. Pass: each click writes a note with that string and
      fret, the playhead steps on, and the notation lane shows the
      frets you clicked. Try it with a capo.
- [ ] **D4 Hearing and live notes.** Click a few frets while stopped,
      then play a MIDI keyboard or guitar. Pass: each click sounds its
      note on the track's instrument (with your interface's latency), and
      the notes you hold down light up on the neck and go out when you
      let go.
- [ ] **D4 Bass and other tunings.** Pick Bass standard and Drop D.
      Pass: the strings and their letters change, and with a tab lane
      the lane's tuning changes too.
