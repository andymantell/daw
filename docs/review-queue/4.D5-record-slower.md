# Phase 4 D5 Record at a slower speed

Play the song slowed down with its pitch kept, record over it, and the
take is sped back up to the song's speed and placed where it was played.
Built at risk on the speed and pitch library choice (0.6): it uses the
practice stretcher we have now, and moves with it if that changes.

- [ ] **D5 A take recorded at 70%.** Load a backing track, arm a track on
      your guitar's input, set **Speed** to 70% on the practice row (or
      Practice → **Slower** six times), turn Record on and play a passage
      along with it. The practice row says "Recording at 70%: takes return
      to the song's speed when you stop". Stop, set Speed back to 100% and
      play. Pass: the take is in time with the backing all the way
      through, at the song's tempo, in tune, and it sounds natural enough
      to keep (listen for smeared pick attacks or a phasey tone, which is
      the stretcher's limit and part of the 0.6 listening).
- [ ] **D5 At 50%.** The same at 50%, the slowest. Pass: as above; say if
      the quality at 50% is good enough to keep a take or only to learn
      with.
- [ ] **D5 With a punch range and a loop.** Speed 75%, Punch bars on (say
      bars 5 to 7, pre-roll and post-roll 1 bar), Loop on round bars 4 to
      8. Pass: each pass makes a take of exactly bars 5 to 7 at the song's
      speed, and with Loop off playback stops by itself after the
      post-roll, as at normal speed.
- [ ] **D5 The take lands in time.** With the recording offset measured
      (1.2), record a click through a loopback cable at 70%. Pass: the
      sped-up clicks sit on the beats within a couple of milliseconds.
- [ ] **D5 Speed from outside the UI.** Practice → Slower, Faster and
      Normal speed and pitch, also found in the command search (Ctrl+K)
      and given keys in Settings → Key commands. Pass: each steps the
      speed by 5% (Normal resets speed and transpose).

## Decisions to accept

- [ ] **Transposed takes go back to the song's key.** Built: with
      Transpose changed while recording, the take is transposed back as
      well as sped up, so it matches the song as it really is. Example:
      a song in E♭ tuning played along in standard tuning at +1 gives a
      take shifted down a semitone. The alternative is to keep the pitch
      as played and only fix the speed. Which do you want?
- [ ] **The slowed recording is kept too.** Built: the input as played
      (file name says `slow`) stays beside the sped-up take in the takes
      folder, so nothing is lost if speeding up goes wrong. It isn't on
      the timeline. Keep it, offer it as a clip, or delete it?
- [ ] **"Keep what I just played" is off at a practice speed.** Built:
      retrospective capture keeps nothing while the song plays slowed.
      Say if you'd like slowed input kept and sped up too.
- [ ] **Punch ranges at a slow speed.** A punch range at a practice speed
      cuts at the bars as played, without moving by the interface's round
      trip, so an edge can be a few milliseconds early (half the round
      trip at 50%). Fine for learning; say if not.
