# 6.1 Audio that follows tempo (first step: stretching between markers)

The engine can now play a clip so that chosen frames of it land on
chosen frames of the timeline, with the audio between stretched evenly
and its pitch kept (`engine/warp.rs`). This is the interface the rest of
the feature, and pitch editing, build on. Nothing is in the app yet.

## Decisions to accept

- [ ] **Built on our own stretcher, at risk.** The speed and pitch
      library is still your choice (0.6 listening). The warp code talks
      to the stretcher through one small interface (`warp::Stretch`:
      set, reset, process, plus "play along this map"), so a library can
      replace our phase vocoder behind it without touching the clips or
      the timeline. If you pick a library that only takes a speed, it
      follows the map block by block instead of frame by frame.
- [ ] **Attacks land within about 4 ms.** Tests put a tied frame within
      4 ms of where it was asked for at stretches of ±25%, and exactly
      at 1:1. A phase vocoder spreads an attack over part of a frame, so
      the loudest point sits a few milliseconds off when the stretch
      changes at that attack. Say if that is audible when you try
      quantising a take; a sharper transient treatment would be the next
      step.
