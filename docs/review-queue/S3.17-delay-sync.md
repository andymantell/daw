# Song test 3, wish 17: delay times in note lengths

The built-in delay has a new setting, **Sync to tempo**. Off (the
default) keeps the time in ms as before. Any other choice times the
echoes as a note length instead: 1/32, 1/16T, 1/16, 1/16., 1/8T, 1/8,
1/8., 1/4T, 1/4, 1/4., 1/2T, 1/2, 1/2. or 1/1 ("T" is a triplet, "." is
dotted). While it is on, the Time slider is replaced by a line saying
which note length it follows.

The time follows the tempo as the song plays, through tempo changes and
glides, and is slowed with the practice speed. The longest echo is now
4 seconds (it was 2), which is a whole note at 60 bpm; slower than that,
a whole note is held to 4 seconds.

Assistants set it with `set_parameter` by name, such as
`"value": "1/8."`; `list_parameters` lists the choices, and gives the
time in ms at the tempo at the play position in a `note`.

## Listening

- [ ] Put a delay on a short sound, set Sync to tempo to 1/8. and the mix
      high, and play over a tempo change: the echoes stay in time on both
      sides of the change. Pass: they land on the grid by ear.
- [ ] Play across a tempo glide with a long delay (1/4 or more): the
      echoes bend in pitch slightly while the time moves, like a tape
      delay. Pass: no clicks or crackles.
